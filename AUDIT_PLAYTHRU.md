## Confidence review — what this audit can't see

- **Single-provider, single-voice, single-blueprint sample.** Everything above is one Sonnet/Howard/ToA run; the quest-silence and location-silence findings (P3, P4) matched the Runelords corpus, so they're likely systemic, but P5 (voice drift) needs a travel-heavy corpus before blaming the plumbing.
- **Harness action bias.** Random suggested-action selection favors the cautious option the model itself proposed — it never types free-text, never rests, never opens menus. Rest/`restSpells`, render, TTS, campaign-switching, and multi-companion paths went unexercised.
- **The pacing question.** 75 turns without leaving the starting city may be correct slow-burn GMing or may be skeleton-act-progression failure — the skeleton block (7.9 KB, injected every turn) produced no visible act pressure. Worth a dedicated look at whether Act 1 → Act 2 transitions ever fire in long campaigns.
- **Companion systems were only negatively exercised** (P2): a sheet-bearing companion in the same run would have shown whether COMPANION tags/XP-mirror actually fire when the sheet exists.
