Pass 1 — Five material corrections

Problem: Declining the paid commission was treated as declining the whole evening.

Change: Voluntary help and ordinary attendance remain available; ending follows the player's decision.

Problem: The midnight payment condition contradicted a legitimately postponed toast.

Change: The agreed postponement now extends the reporting deadline wherever the offer is stated.

Problem: Devrin could not produce an annex handed to him because the finale only recognized the coat.

Change: Use actual custody and access, and preserve copies and witnessed evidence after destruction.

Problem: The timed body discovery required Brix even if he was absent, detained or dead.

Change: An available attendant discovers the body; the host assigns the response without reviving actors.

Problem: The no-intervention ending froze NPC initiative and ignored evidence they might themselves discover.

Change: The host secures the scene, hears witnesses and can postpone the ceremony; the outcome follows known evidence.

Change: Forbid resurrection for plot convenience; honor established powers and let earned changes affect events.
