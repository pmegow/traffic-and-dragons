What changed

  1. One consistent crime: Devrin plants a false tariff annex on Petrell. The genuine treaty stays in its chest.
  2. A complete cast and murder: Orris is the fifth guest and is already dead; Mira is the scribe and planting witness, not an invented murder eyewitness.
  3. Independent evidence: the coat and authenticated commission can expose the fraud even when Mira leaves or dies. Murder proof is separate.
  4. A clear invitation and job at 21:00, public event cues and a short route through two investigation locations.
  5. Early success, denial, escape, a quiet settlement, mistaken accusation, missed midnight and partial success all have consequences.
  6. All seven NPCs use the existing no-plot-armor setting. Losing a delegate has an explicit consequence instead of forcing an escape or a signature.
