The first five minutes

  1. Sign in. Press Sign in and approve the popup — Google or GitHub, whichever you already have. During the beta the operator's account pays for every turn; there is no key to buy and nothing to paste.
  2. Pick a way in. The Home page (File ▸ 🏠 Home) has the curated stories and your library. Start a campaign there, or build a hero of your own in the six-step character wizard — there are no wrong answers, and a sentence or two of backstory is plenty.
  3. Say what you do. Type it in plain words in the box at the bottom and press Send — "I ask the innkeeper about the missing caravan." Tap a suggested action to drop it into the box; hold it to send at once. The dashed fourth button is the game's own suggestion, built from your sheet.
  4. Roll your own dice. When something is chancy the Game Master names the check and you roll — the result is final, and failure moves the story too. You can hand the dice back under File ▸ ⚙ Settings ▸ 📖 Narrative options.
  5. Rest when you are hurt. Say so in the story — the dashed fourth button offers it too. A rest restores party health and mana to full, moves the clock to dawn and makes camp — the place you return to if you die.
  6. You do not simply die. At 0 HP you are down, not dead: struggle or yield, and the story resolves it within two turns. A true death brings Death, who walks you back to your last camp. Three returns per campaign; the fourth death ends the story with a written ending.
  7. Close the app when you are done. Your game is saved on this device and, signed in, on your account — it follows you between devices and you resume exactly where you left off.
  8. In the car: File ▸ 🚗 Car Mode reads the story aloud, listens for your spoken action and speaks the numbered options so you can answer by number.
  9. Something wrong? File ▸ ⚠ Report bug… — the form shows exactly what it sends before you send it.
