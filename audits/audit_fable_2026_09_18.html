Fable audit — 2026-09-18

Traffic and Dragons at v1.949 (commit 08a5260). A deep read-only technical review for drift vulnerabilities, redundancies and vestigial processes. Nothing was fixed. Every entry below was double-checked by the auditor against the source, and where a behaviour was claimed it was reproduced live in the headless engine (dev/load-engine.js) before being logged. Candidates that turned out to serve a purpose are listed in the rejected ledger at the end.

Verdict. The core anti-drift stack holds: the tag registry discipline is intact (every handler stripped, every strip name handled or registered), the transcript is never trimmed anywhere, the stable prompt half is pure, and the engine manifest matches index.html exactly. The holes are at the edges of that stack, and they cluster into six classes: (1) kind gates that only some handlers honour (the village pays XP, buries residents and hands companions goods that the hero is refused); (2) parsers that accept a malformed or duplicated tag and say nothing; (3) node-record carriers that each remember a different subset of the schema (thirteen hand-rolled node literals, a merge that drops floor plans, a split that drops owners); (4) UI paths that write canon around the filers or around the busy gate; (5) sync paths that bypass the CAS guard or persist a blank on a parse failure; (6) contracts that pin the wrong file, the wrong spelling, or nothing. Counts: 105 findings — 27 high, 38 medium, 40 low; by class 47 drift holes, 14 silent failures, 19 redundancies, 16 vestigial, 9 documentation rot.

Method

  • Seven parallel read-only evidence sweeps (tags, prompt assembly, memory/identity, state/sync, game/UI, voice/audio, dev tooling/docs), each told to search for the purpose a candidate might serve before reporting it, and to list what it rejected.
  • Auditor's own passes: a dead-symbol census over 1,748 top-level definitions (comments and strings stripped, HTML-built handlers counted), an empty-catch census, a direct-canon-write scan, a tag vocabulary cross-check (handlers vs strip list vs every bracketed name in engine text), a node-literal census.
  • Verification: 26 behavioural claims reproduced by script in the headless engine; 60 textual claims checked mechanically against the cited lines; the rest read by eye. Two sweep claims were corrected during verification (the location merge does fold node fields, through locFoldNodeRecords; the sibling-place refusal in #415 is caught by the resolver, not the filer) and are recorded in their corrected form.
  • Scope limits: no live GM calls were made; Car Mode, Bluetooth and the Piper soak were read, not driven; the Fly.dev server is out of scope.

highdrift hole

repro
