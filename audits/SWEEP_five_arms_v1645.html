The Korrag Reckoning

Five arms head to head on the same 50-turn standard campaign: both Gemini reasoning budgets, both Claude tiers, and the ChatGPT model. Built entirely from corpora already on disk — no new money was spent to make this page.

This page answers one question the Korrag Menu could not: how much of a model's quality comes from the model, and how much from how hard it is allowed to think? The Gemini pair below is the same model at two reasoning budgets, which turns an untested assumption into a measurement.

The two contract halves. Every arm is judged on both, because the sweep already proved they are separable capabilities — gemini-3.5-flash held tag syntax perfectly while its prose resurrected the dead.

① Tag fidelity — does the engine's hidden state vocabulary land? Measured as zero-tag turns, distinct/total tags, and unknown tags. ② Canon obedience — does the prose respect the state it was handed? Measured as the dead-actor scan, death-tag timing, and premise retention.

The bottom line

what the five arms actually say

All five arms hold both contract halves. The capability threshold established by the sweep is real and every model here clears it. What separates them is not correctness but character: how much prose they write, how densely they tag, how fast they answer, and what they cost.

The reasoning budget turned out to be nearly free. Dropping gemini-3.7-flash from mandatory thinking to thinkingLevel:"low" cost nothing measurable on either contract half — and the low arm is the one without the premature death-tag blemish. It also got faster, cheaper, and wordier, because thinking-on was spending its output budget deliberating instead of narrating. This was adopted in v1.645.

The premium tier buys structure, not correctness. opus-5 is the only arm to reach level 3 and the archetype gate, files 53 distinct tags against everyone else's 32–39, and writes 1,544 characters a turn — more than four times gpt-5.6-sol. It is also roughly three times sonnet-5's cost, which is why it stays unlisted.

The reasoning-budget finding

the one axis this page adds

gemini-3.7-flash thinks mandatorily and bills the thinking as output. Until v1.644 the meter could not see it, so the cost was real but invisible. Because the two Gemini arms ran under different meters, their saved totals are not comparable — the lever had to be measured directly, with paired calls on one identical 17,305-token live gameplay prompt:

The counter-intuitive column is visible output: the low arm writes more prose. Thinking-on was spending roughly 275 tokens per call deliberating and then narrating less. Across the full runs the same pattern holds at the story level — 727 characters a turn against 647.

And the quality did not pay for it. The low arm matched or beat its thinking-on sibling on every measured axis, including the one that matters most: the premature death-tag blemish belongs to the thinking-ON arm, not the low one. Premise retention was also better — the campaign's central "soul-forge" thread appears in 9 turns of the low run against a single turn of the thinking-on run.

Voice, side by side

same Howard directive · same campaign · first sword-stroke of each run

All five ran the Robert E. Howard prose voice. The directive asks for concrete verbs of motion and violence, one vivid image per sentence, relentlessly propulsive.

Steel rasps against scabbard as your longsword clears leather in a blinding arc of tempered iron. You sidestep the wild slash and drive the heavy point deep beneath his rusted breastplate…gemini-3.7-flash · thinking on
Your longsword cuts a savage arc through the freezing night, shearing clean through the iron plates of Aldric's pauldron and biting deep into the meat of his shoulder.gemini-3.7-flash · thinking low
Steel hisses free of the scabbard, and you close the distance at a wary crouch, sword low, eyes cutting the scene apart piece by piece.claude-sonnet-5
The blade comes free with a rasp that cuts the wind, and then you are running. Ash sprays under your boots. The trail drops and the wreck rises to meet you…claude-opus-5
The net snaps through empty air as you hurl yourself beneath its weighted rim. You surge from the ash and crash into Aldric Saltborn, your longsword biting through his leather guard…gpt-5.6-sol

The two Gemini arms are stylistically indistinguishable — further evidence the reasoning budget is not buying voice. sonnet-5 and opus-5 both push toward their own house rhythm (sonnet's appraising crouch, opus's staccato momentum) rather than pure pastiche, matching the owner's earlier live judgment that Gemini's Howard reads more like Howard. gpt-5.6-sol carries the vocabulary in a clipped tactical cadence.

What this page does not establish

the honest limits

Different engine versions. The arms ran between v1.638 and v1.644. Prompt content, nudges, and guards moved between those builds. This is a comparison of arms as they were run, not a controlled same-build experiment.

The metering asterisk. v1.644 folded Gemini's thoughtsTokenCount into output. Arms before it under-report Gemini output. The paired A/B exists precisely because the saved totals could not settle it — and its thinking-on side is n=5 against n=2, because a Google 503 load-shedding storm ate samples.

Three of five costs are estimates. MODEL_PRICING carries Claude entries only. sonnet-5's $2.64 is priced and verified; opus-5, gpt-5.6-sol and both Gemini figures are estimates awaiting real pricing entries.

One campaign, one tone, one voice, one seed. The standing #22 field-validation width caveat applies to every arm here equally. A model that suits Howard sword-and-sorcery has not thereby been shown to suit political intrigue.

Depth reached is not purely merit. opus-5's level 3 required a human to pick the archetype mid-run — the harness cannot yet answer that modal. Progression depth partly reflects how combat-heavy each random action walk happened to be.

Where this leaves the menu

no ruling implied

Nothing here argues for changing the shipping menu, and The Korrag Menu is untouched — it remains owner-ruled. What this page adds is that the cheapest seat on that menu just got measurably cheaper and faster at no cost to either contract half, and that the reasoning-budget knob is now a known, tested dimension rather than an assumption.

The open follow-ups are unchanged from the sweep: real MODEL_PRICING entries for opus-5, gpt-5.6-sol and gemini-3.7-flash; the callGM fetch timeout (#29); and a harness modal-handler so a run can cross the archetype gate unattended.
