What was checked, and what a flag means

Audit snapshot: 4 October 2026, after applying the approved names. All 507 capability effect descriptions were compared with the complete official SRD 5.1 and 5.2.1 PDFs. The scan matches contiguous sequences of at least six words, ignoring case, punctuation, ligatures and line-break hyphenation; it retains the longest match per source. Shorter matches and paraphrases can be missed. Evidence quotes below use normalized spacing and capitalization. Page links use PDF page numbers. There are no identical full descriptions within this capability collection after normalization.

17 descriptions merit a closer wording or provenance review, 42 have lower-priority shared rules phrasing, and 448 have no active flag. Of those 448, 417 had no flagged match and 31 were excluded on 5 October 2026 because their matches concern duration, turn timing or effect-ending conditions. Timing evidence is also excluded from mixed findings, while their other wording concerns remain. Four of the 17 have targeted evidence outside those SRDs; two rely on secondary transcriptions and say so explicitly. This is a bounded textual screening, not an exhaustive search of published works or legal clearance. Source claims concern wording overlap, not infringement. Matching turn timing or numeric mechanics alone is weak evidence of copied expression.

The official SRDs are available under CC BY 4.0. A matching SRD passage may call for correct attribution rather than a rewrite. Other publications need their own provenance and license review. Changing a capability name does not change the provenance of its description.

Rewrite guidance: preserve the existing costs, targets, ranges, durations, saving throws, numbers and exceptions unless you intend a game-design change. Mechanics are shown under each current description. Rephrase from the intended behavior; a synonym swap may leave the same distinctive structure.
