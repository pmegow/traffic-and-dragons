SAY-only companion growth

7 October 2026 · v1.1163 · follow-up to the two #437 fixes.

Cause and correction

COMPANION_GROWTH stripped every bracketed tag before looking for the companion's name. This erased SAY attribution, so speech identified only by its speaker tag could not settle a purpose, birth a new one, or change a flaw. Early refusals appeared only on the console.

The shared companionGrowthOnScreen predicate accepts a speaker in this reply when the existing canonical companion resolver identifies the same sheet, with aliases, case variations and optional mood supported. An end-of-reply cast omission does not erase dialogue that happened earlier in the response. Growth's own tag, other metadata and stored presence cannot authorize a change.

The prose fallback uses word boundaries and permits a first name only when it is unique among the hero and recorded NPC names; articles and shared first names are not evidence. Dead companions receive no growth. Every rejected growth produces a visible warning line naming the reason. The tag vocabulary and prompt text are unchanged.

Failure-first evidence

Three new tests failed before implementation: SAY-only settlement produced only a presence receipt; wrong-speaker refusal lacked a warning; unknown-companion refusal produced no mutation receipt. An independent in-memory replay on the Necrotic Dungeon turn 35 save also failed: Daeris spoke the settlement but retained her creditor motivation. All now pass.
