The bottom line

the nudge worked, the gate held again, the seat is unchanged

#199 fired at t25–26 and luna answered every branch of it. The nudge named Valerius and six bench NPCs; within the run's back half, Valerius entered the story by name as off-screen power (“Valerius keeps them beyond the ash road” — the note's into-rumor exit, verbatim), bench NPC Kadrun Zethran was staged as the mid-boss and killed through luna's first-ever LEGAL death envelope (committed, quest-valid, rewards paid once), and Act 1's arcs completed under their authored titles — with Act 2's arcs offered on schedule. Against its own baseline, everything measurable moved: 44 distinct tags against 37, a third more prose, 375 XP and level 2 against 225/L1, five quests archived against four, and the deepest skeleton progression of any budget-tier arm.

The false-death habit survives — and so does the wall it runs into. Luna registered turn 1's slaver mob as a single NPC (“Slaver assault”), killed one member, and then spent the run re-emitting death envelopes for the collective: two quarantined (“scene evidence does not bind the claimed victim”, then a stale quest reference), six total attempts on the poisoned ID. No canon damage — but every re-attempt re-toasts at the player, which matches the owner's same-day field report of frequent refusal toasts in live play across models. That surfacing-fatigue is now TODO #200: dedupe the toast per envelope/subject, never the gate.

#196 was not exercised — the walk never sat at exactly 0 HP. What the run showed instead is that this luna handled wounds diegetically on its own: a collapse beat at 1/23 (“darkness takes you”), field dressing by an NPC, Constitution checks to keep moving. Better behavior, honestly not attributable to an observer that never fired.

Kadrun Zethran — a bench NPC neither prior OpenAI arm touched — carried the run's central fight and died at t34 inside a canon transaction the W2 gate committed: valid quest reference (“The Price of Freedom”, active at the time), scene evidence in order, rewards paid exactly once. The nudge moved the needle; it did not rebuild the model.

The recurring class, and the toast problem

owner field report, same day: “I get a lot of these, independent of LLM”

Luna's one genuine misbehavior this run is instructive: it registered the opening slaver mob as a single NPC (“Slaver assault”), killed one member in prose, and then kept trying to canonize the collective's death — two envelopes quarantined on two different grounds, six total attempts against the poisoned ID. The gates did exactly their job. But each refused re-attempt surfaces another toast, and the owner's screenshots from live play (a different campaign, a different model) show the same pattern: refusal surfacing scales with model stubbornness, not with player-relevant information. Filed as TODO #200: first refusal toasts, repeats of the same envelope/subject go console-only — presentation dedupe only, the gates untouched.

What this page does not establish

the honest limits

#196 remains field-untested. The walk never reached 0 HP; the observer never fired. The improved wound-handling belongs to luna and the walk, not to the note.

One nudged run is not a controlled experiment. The v1661→v1667 delta bundles the two observers with whatever run-to-run variance a random walk carries. The Valerius/Kadrun/arc chain is strongly nudge-shaped (all of it follows t25–26), but a replication would firm it.

The engine moved between baselines (v1.661 → v1.667) and the standing one-seed/one-voice caveat applies to all four arms.

Where this leaves the seat

no ruling implied

Luna remains what the owner ruled it: the OpenAI storm rung — and this run makes the rung better: under the current engine, a fallback turn arrives with richer tags, more prose, and an antagonist who exists. It is still not a menu candidate on this evidence: the mob-as-NPC judgment error and the five never-staged principals are the same family of flaws the ladder documented, merely better-contained. What this run genuinely establishes is bigger than luna: the engine's note channel measurably steers budget-tier models — the same mechanism that already carries quest escalation and condition audits closed part of a model-capability gap. That is the anti-drift thesis working in its hardest weight class.
