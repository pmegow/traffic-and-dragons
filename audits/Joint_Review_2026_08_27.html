③ The joint P0 list

13 items · every one either found by both reviews, verified ×2 by Fable, or Fable-verified from Sol's evidence this session

  • JP0-1 · Close the act-level exits and stamp under parallel acts — one lifecycle design
    highdriftBOTH

    Sol P0-01 + Sol P1-04 · Fable f0 + Fable f7

    Both reviews independently found that the arc wall's act-level boundary is open: ACT_COMPLETE ignores its operand (Sol's runtime repro — "[ACT_COMPLETE:Totally Wrong]" closes the first active act), closes an act with live child arcs, sweeps nothing, and strands their stamped progeny in permanent immunity; and in a PARALLEL act (2+ live arcs) nothing is stamped at all — the phase that covered ~45% of the owner's own campaign.

    Merged accept conditions: a wrong/malformed act title mutates nothing and warns loudly; an act cannot complete while a child arc is live unless the transition explicitly resolves that arc and its stamped descendants; no completed act ever contains an active arc; when the arc is ambiguous but the act is not, the quest is stamped with the ACT (bornAct) and swept at act completion — preserving the no-guessing principle; the no-active-parent gap (last arc closed, act not yet transitioned — Sol P1-04) either waits, attaches to the act, or refuses loudly; parallel semantics are decided by act.parallel, not by the live-arc COUNT side effect (Sol's probe and a Fable verifier both confirmed a parallel act whittled to one live arc silently resumes stamping); valid final ARC+ACT co-emission stays safe and the non-final co-emission case (Sol: leaves two active act/arc branches) is mutation-tested. Also: ARC_COMPLETE parses only the FIRST tag per response (Fable f6) — a parallel act closing two arcs in one response sweeps only one; fix inside the same pass.

  • JP0-2 · Give the wall a post-sweep prompt channel
    highdriftFABLE

    Fable f8 (verified ×2)

    After the wall closes a thread, the crisis line still demands the GM register any pursued goal, the blocked |active re-creation is silently swallowed, and no note says the thread was closed — so the GM lands in an untracked-thread state, the exact blindness #231 exists to end.

    A wall-variant 2-turn note naming the swept titles with closed-with-the-arc wording — NOT the #229 recentAbandon block, whose "abandoned by the player" text is false for a wall sweep and would steer the GM to narrate a walking-away that never happened.

  • JP0-3 · Close the quest state machine: status validation, the declined hole, and archive-overwrite protection
    highdriftBOTH — elevated in joint

    Sol P0-04 · Fable f54 + Fable f17 + Fable F1

    Sol's runtime probes elevate what Fable filed as three low rows into one P0 bundle: an improvised status like "paused" creates a zombie row invisible to every prompt reader and tooth; a DECLINED title can be resurrected because the reopen guard covers abandoned/completed/failed but not declined; and a later archive write overwrites the title-keyed history row — including (Fable's trace) the paid-rewards receipt that blocks double-pay.

    One explicit status-transition table for every writer; invalid status refused loudly; declined joins the reopen guard (owner decides declined/reoffer semantics); an archive write over an existing terminal record preserves it (disambiguated key or field-preserving fold — the paid and objectives fields must survive); every stored live status visible to prompt, health, UI, export, and Table Talk readers; sabotage proves the rejection and history cases. Fable adjudication note: Fable rated the zombie-status row low in isolation — Sol's declined-resurrection repro plus the overwrite class is what makes the bundle P0; the pieces share one handler and one design pass.

  • JP0-4 · Never silently replace corrupt recall stores with blank state
    highrecallSOL — Fable-verified

    Sol P0-02 · state.js:476-478

    Corrupt sessionLog JSON silently becomes [] and corrupt memory JSON silently becomes blankMemory() at load — no warning, no rescue of the original bytes — and the next save persists the blank over whatever was recoverable, while the campaign looks healthy.

    Fable verified the mechanism first-hand this session: the E73 per-key try/catch design (a corrupt side key must not discard a good worldState) is right, but the catch arms are silent and destroy evidence. Mirror the transcript's UA3 pattern: stash the original bytes in a bounded rescue record, toast the degraded state, and do not persist a blank until the player chooses. Honesty caveat recorded: localStorage setItem is atomic per spec, so the realistic corruption vectors (hand edits, storage-layer bugs, cleaners) are narrower than the loss class suggests — the P0 rests on the no-silent-failures policy plus total-tier loss plus the cheapness of mirroring an existing pattern.

  • JP0-5 · Archive keys must round-trip imports by registry, not hand-copied allowlist
    highrecallSOL — Fable-verified

    Sol P0-03 · ui-files.js:477 · tag_table.js:517-518

    The death-retraction preimages (memory.archive.npcDeathCorrections) are silently dropped by every .tnd export/import round-trip because the import allowlist was hand-copied before the key existed — and this is at least the FOURTH archive key this same allowlist has dropped (attitudeSpec, eras, the #144A trio, now this).

    Fable verified: the writer exists (tag_table.js:517-518), the key is absent from the ui-files.js:477 whitelist, and the whitelist's own comment credits "Sol's find, drift audit 2026-08-08" for a PRIOR instance of the identical class. The project's own second-instance rule says enumerate the class, don't ship a point fix: replace the allowlist with a single archive-key registry (or carry-unknown-keys semantics) that import, the "full archive" test, and any future consumer all derive from — a failing round-trip fixture first, per Sol's accept condition.
