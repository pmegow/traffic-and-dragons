Questions to resolve before implementation

Commune with Nature

Reference conflict · key: commune with nature

The chosen Fauna rewrite deals +3d8 force, but the dice field still says +1d6. Flora adds a STR save, restraint and a rooted caster, while the save field is N/A. Preserve the rewrite choice and reconcile these reference fields when implementing.

Dancing Lights

Reference conflict · key: dancing lights

The rewrite creates up to six lights; the targets field still allows four. The humanoid-combination option also remains, although the original torch-sized-light wording is gone.

Flame Strike

Reference conflict · key: flame strike

The rewrite specifies a 10-foot diameter, while the targets field specifies a 10-foot radius and 40-foot height. Choose the intended footprint before implementation; these are different areas.

Inflict Wounds

Rule clarification · key: inflict wounds

The rewrite adds damage to the next successful melee attack rather than making an immediate melee spell attack. Its duration is still instantaneous. Specify how long the charge lasts and whether it applies to weapon attacks before implementation.

Extra Attack

Rule clarification · key: extra attack

One extra attack per round does not say which action grants it or whether it stacks with Double Attack. The previous wording tied two attacks to taking the attack action. This is a rules change to confirm, not a spelling correction.

Silence

Rule clarification · key: silence

The rewrite says a 20ft sphere without specifying radius or diameter; the targets field says 20ft radius. It also expands negation from thunder damage to all sonic attacks. Preserve the choice, but make the intended area and scope explicit.

Hide from Undead

Internal contradiction · key: hide from undead

The opening promises complete undetectability, but the next sentence allows intelligent undead a save. Qualify the opening so it does not overrule that exception. The original sensory wording is removed; remaining parallels mostly describe the same rules.

Wall of Thorns

Duplicate name · key: wall of thorns

There is another capability keyed wall of thorns (one space). This two-space entry is 2d6/STR/4 hours; the other is 7d8/DEX/concentration. They must remain separate until you choose which to keep or give them distinct names. Removing the extra space would create a collision. This entry also targets self despite describing a wall at range.

Wall of Thorns

Duplicate name · key: wall of thorns

A separate two-space wall of thorns entry has different damage, saves and duration. Decide whether these are two distinct abilities or which definition is authoritative before merging or renaming.

Sleep

Internal contradiction · key: sleep

The effect starts with the least wounded, while the targets field says weakest first and the HP-pool rule depends on remaining hit points. Specify the intended order; do not silently swap these meanings.

Augury

Remaining descriptive overlap · key: augury

The rewrite still uses Weal/Woe and their combined omen categories. The sentences are rephrased, but that was the original descriptive concern. Keep deliberately with an attribution decision, or replace the presentation if independence is the goal. Timing is excluded.

Druidic

Remaining phrase overlap · key: druidic

The revised sentence still contains the six-word sequence Druidic, the secret language of druids in both SRDs. This is a short definition, not evidence by itself of infringement. Record a deliberate keep/attribution decision or rephrase if desired.
