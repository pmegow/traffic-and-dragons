Recommended decision

Retain the underlying legacy recipe and feature identity for inspection. Publish no hut label, footprint, hit target, ownership implication or interior. If reliable prior narration or an explicit player confirmation is later supplied, admit only its supported attributes with that source.

Absence from this turn-7 record is not proof that the hut never appeared in unavailable turns. It is enough to withhold it conservatively now.
