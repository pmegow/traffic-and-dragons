The village holds as built. The mode, the peace, the residents and the swap all behaved as the record says, in the fiction and in the engine, and the state survived a reload.

The Game Master opened on a person and a change with no quest and no threat. Two deliberate attempts at violence, a stabbing and a bar fight called with "roll for initiative", were intercepted in prose exactly as the Pax clause asks, with no harm or combat tag emitted. A synthetic probe proved the engine gates on their own: HP, companion HP, combat start and a reported death were refused with the registry copy, a loose scene death and a bare death claim were dropped, and healing still landed. Whispers fired at turn 2 from the resident pool, about a resident, distorted the way the note allows. The free hero swap put Tobin in the street as a resident and Daeris behind "you", with the switch-of-view block honoured on the very next turn. Four things carry into the E/F build and one transport gap is filed. None is a contract break.

How the residents got there

Sign-in is GitHub or Google OAuth, which the agent cannot perform, so the owner signed in and clicked New Campaign. The library read went to the real endpoint and returned the account's zero characters; three fixture heroes lifted from the turn-2097 Runelords save were merged through the same importVillageResidents core. The library write-back was replaced by a log-only twin for the run, so the hero swap could never post a fixture hero into the account's library.

Findings to carry into the build

  1. The adventure default weather leaks into the village and colours every turn

    The new-game path seeds "cold wind carrying ash"; the village blueprint has no weather field, so the Game Master echoed it at turn 0 and wrote ash into all seven turns. A home with no dangers should not open under a burning sky. Remedy: an opening weather on the kind registry, adventure pinned.

    → #6, phase E/F pre-build list

  2. The demoted hero gets no house

    Importing a resident mints a house node with an owner; the swap's demotion does not, so Tobin has no house after the swap. Phase E's stash needs the node the moment a hero becomes a resident.

    → #6, phase E

  3. An NPC tag downgrades a resident to "acquaintance"

    Frizwick and Morwen both lost their resident relationship label on their first NPC tag. Nothing reads the label for the village, so this is cosmetic today, but the roster shows "acquaintance" for a hero the player once was. Remedy: keep the label when the resident flag is set.

    → #6

  4. Trade happened at a doorstep and a hearth, with no named shopkeeper

    Wares from Frizwick's door at t1, coin for stew fetched "from whoever's tending the fire" at t5. Expected until phase F, which limits buy and sell to a shop sub-location and names both parties. Recorded as the baseline F's acceptance test must flip.

    → #6, phase F

  5. The gateway's error responses carry no CORS headers

    The first call after the swap failed and the browser could only report a CORS block; the status and body were unreadable. The action was kept and the retry got a 200 with a 93 KB body, so the cause is still unknown. That is the gap: every server-side failure is unattributable on the client.

    → new row #406

Minor observations, no action

  • The rumour called Ammut, Daeris's former hero and companion, "some devil or demon". No event was invented, but a person's nature was recast. Worth watching when the whisper pool is the players' own heroes.
  • Prose time drift: "tending the fire tonight" and "a tavern at midday" at 8:50 in the morning; "before dark takes it" at 9:10, seeded by the player's own "tonight". One model, one session.
  • Fixture artefacts: Daeris at L11 with no archetype triggered the level-3 archetype modal on reload, and a "rest to claim Lv 13" toast. A real library hero would carry its archetype. The summariser ran at t3 and t6, ordinary for this prose length.
  • The handoff's "sign in with a throwaway account" is not a path the agent can take. The owner signs in and clicks New Campaign; the agent drives the rest.
