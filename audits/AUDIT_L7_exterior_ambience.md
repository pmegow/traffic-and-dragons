# Village exterior ambience — v1.931

The owner asked to roll in the reviewed recordings and explicitly selected “Implement ambience and transitions.” Their export has 26 approvals, 20 rejections and three unreviewed gravel footsteps; they confirmed that no comments were entered. The exact export and approved asset index live under ignored Audio/Reviews and Audio/approved-audio-library.json. All source files remain available, including rejected ones for reference.

The village exterior follows committed location and clock state: morning 05:00–10:00, day 10:00–18:00, light-wind evening 18:00–21:00, night 21:00–05:00. Midnight and exact boundaries are covered. Only a recorded world node with no current sublocation counts as outside. An unknown interior cannot inherit outdoor sound. The existing canonical smithy/hours rule remains intact. No game state, model prompt, tag parser or saved campaign format changed.

The old controller stopped its only source before loading another, causing the reported hard exit. It now retains at most a current and an outgoing voice. Scene changes fade for three seconds; rapid return can reuse and reverse the outgoing voice. A third change releases the oldest voice. A single abortable load/decode slot uses generation checks, so stale completions cannot play. The outgoing voice fades even if the destination fails or loads slowly; a cold/slow download can therefore leave a gap before the incoming fade. Disabled/held/paused/hidden-idle/non-gameplay/campaign changes dispose immediately. Timer retirement releases source, gain nodes and buffer references.

Each voice has separate scene-envelope and mix gains. Narration ducks both by about 10 dB without cancelling scene fades. Microphone capture clears gain automation and sets both mix gains directly to zero before recognition starts. Browser testing found that scheduling zero at currentTime alone did not yield a synchronous zero at capture start; the direct gain assignment fixes that exact failure. Voice/capture events retain the existing additive subscriptions and document car-intent contract.

Four long approved recordings are prepared as mono MP3 loops, preserving their full duration except a two-second tail/head overlap. RMS/peak level matching and provenance are in sfx/village-exterior-provenance.json. The morning rooster is retained; no claim of removal. The owner approved the source selections but did not mark these four clean, so the prepared loops still need listening acceptance. Morning/day/night are approximately 108 seconds; evening 121 seconds. Music, animals, footsteps, doors and smithing effects are catalogued as approved but are not triggered in this rollout.
