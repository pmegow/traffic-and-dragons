Temperaments

What each survivor is FOR

Sonnet 5 — the default, and the right one. Fastest by a factor of two, cleanest corrective instincts, best premise retention, mid-band price. The reference experience.

Opus 5 — the storyteller’s tier. Everything Sonnet does plus depth nobody else showed: the only arm to reach level 3 and the archetype gate, twice the XP flow, a three-quest web, ten filed chapters, resolved future-events, and prose that treats a looted falchion as a thirty-turn memory. It costs roughly 3× Sonnet and needs its price verified — a premium tier in the literal sense.

Kadrun Zethran’s hand was on this grip six hours ago. He shouted the odds on your life for three winters and now his blade rides your hip.opus-5, turn 42 — drift avoidance as storytelling

Gemini 3.7 Flash — the budget seat. A fifth of Sonnet’s price, strong voice, one self-healing blemish on record. The economy option that doesn’t feel like one.

GPT-5.6 Sol — mechanically rich, stylistically flat. Holds everything, sings nothing. Kept for players who bring their OpenAI account; not the recommendation.

Bring-up debts this benchmark paid: opus-5’s adaptive thinking initially made every response parse as empty (fixed v1.642 — thinking disabled across the Claude-5 family, parser hardened); gpt-5.x rejects max_tokens (fixed v1.640); and the opus arm ran the account dry mid-run — the credit-exhaustion path failed loud and correct in the app, and the harness’s auto-retry self-resumed the run on top-up.
