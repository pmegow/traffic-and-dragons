The assessment

Cassivel has a convincing physical identity and a strong image at its center: a city that flies because it is a restraint. Several revisions already give the final disaster concrete consequences. The principal risks are that successful player tactics are cancelled by absolute rules, every relationship becomes a debt trap, and the finale introduces another invisible hand after naming the mastermind.

What to preserve

Keep the engineering horrorCopper tags, boiler galleries, the memory-scrubbing workshop and the leviathan make the setting feel like one connected place.

Keep the Kolm fallbackKolm’s secret explicitly survives their death through records or Vessa. This is a good model for other essential revelations.

Keep Sump Row specificThe Falling Ward names a vulnerable district rather than treating thousands of lives as an abstract number. Let people saved there matter during evacuation.

Improvements worth making

High: resolve before relying on the affected scene in a player-facing playtest. Medium: a concrete improvement to clarity, agency or payoff. These are editorial priorities, not measured gameplay failure rates.

Keep important rules and creature weaknesses inside the loader limits

Why this improves play

The real designer loader cut the two rules from 533 and 510 characters to 400, and Boiler-Choked notes from 928 to 800. The published text and the playable import therefore differ; the tag-removal finishing condition disappears and two rules end mid-thought. A successful validation result does not catch this loss.

Source evidence · 3 excerpts
rules[0]“the GM should treat any such reading as automatic detection with no roll”
rules[1]“during a later scene where the player's and the ally's interests diverge”
creatures[1].notes“snap it, and the corpse goes still”
Recommended change

Edit the content to fit the existing limits. Split independent rules into short, complete entries. Put the Boiler-Choked’s methods of defeat before atmosphere and trim repeated setting description. Review the rule meanings in the following findings before preserving them.

Suggested wording or scene · proposal, not existing canon

Proposed creature replacement: Boiler-Choked are purged workers animated by furnace steam and fused copper tags. Slow in open space, they grapple in smoke-filled corridors. Coolant quenches their steam; removing and snapping the throat-tag stills the corpse. Show both weaknesses before a cramped encounter. Their presence exposes a recent purge.

How to verify the improvement

Open the revised blueprint in the designer, export it, and compare these fields byte-for-byte. Every weakness and operative condition should survive.

High priority · 2 of 6

Let a convincing forged tag work until something exposes it

Why this improves play

The rule offers forgery as an escape tactic and then makes every observer automatically see through it. The creature entry separately supports tag spoofing. A player can invest in a plausible plan and learn only afterward that success was impossible.

Source evidence · 3 excerpts
rules[0]“the player cannot simply hide their status without removing or forging it”
rules[0]“Any faction operative who reads a forged or absent tag immediately exposes the player”
creatures[0].notes“spoof it by swapping tags with another indenture”
Recommended change

Distinguish a missing tag, a visibly crude forgery, a plausible forged record, and a matching stolen tag. Give officials an actual means of detection, such as a close audit or contradictory assignment. Alert the player when that scrutiny is imminent.

Suggested wording or scene · proposal, not existing canon

Proposed short rule: A missing or visibly invalid tag invites immediate detention. A convincing forged or borrowed tag can pass ordinary inspection. A detailed audit may expose a mismatch; show what the auditor checks and allow the player to distract, flee, bargain or supply corroborating records.

How to verify the improvement

Try the same checkpoint with no tag, a crude forgery and a carefully matched stolen tag. The outcomes should differ for stated reasons.

High priority · 3 of 6

Make the purge survivable through the player’s actions

Why this improves play

The prescribed rescue makes Vessa’s intervention the only authored success even if the player exposes the purge, escapes through a duct or rescues fellow prisoners. Grim circumstances can constrain choices without making the player a spectator at their own survival.

Source evidence · 2 excerpts
acts[0].arcs[2].dnaHint“not a fight you win by strength but by being worth more alive to a watcher who intervenes at the last second”
npcs[0].notes“she buys the player's indenture to spare them the culling execution”
Recommended change

Retain Vessa’s offer as one route. Add an escape route through the machinery and an exposure route using the clerk’s evidence. Let her buy the contract afterward if that follows events; she should not erase a successful escape to reenact the rescue.

Suggested wording or scene · proposal, not existing canon

Proposed arc instruction: Vessa offers protection at the price of service. The party may accept, escape through the ventilation shafts, or disrupt the purge by making its evidence public. Each route ends the immediate culling threat and reveals that someone deliberately arranged the indenture, with different allies and pursuers afterward.

How to verify the improvement

Play through the purge with the player refusing Vessa. The act must still reach an earned outcome without mandatory recapture.

High priority · 4 of 6

Identify the final manipulator once

Why this improves play

Ferrin’s dossier names him as the true hand, while his confrontation hints that someone else was always behind him. The claim that all three armies were never enemies also undercuts the faction conflict without establishing a shared command. The player’s investigation can feel like a moving target.

Source evidence · 3 excerpts
acts[2].arcs[1].dnaHint“Your patron is neither savior nor mastermind but a middle-piece like you”
npcs[4].secret“He is the true hand moving the pieces”
acts[2].arcs[0].dnaHint“three armies who were never really enemies to each other, only to you”
Recommended change

Make Ferrin the author of the manufactured indenture and keep the Meridian’s containment agenda independent. Clarify that Vessa is the subordinate patron. Different factions can exploit the same victim without secretly being one organization.

Suggested wording or scene · proposal, not existing canon

Proposed confrontation: Vessa acquired the contract on Ferrin’s orders. Ferrin engineered the debt to place the party near the core and profit from the crisis. The Meridian neither serves him nor created the debt; its own ruthless containment plan now collides with his ambition. No further hidden mastermind replaces these motives.

How to verify the improvement

After Act Three, the player should be able to name who caused the debt, who bought it, and why the Meridian allowed the theft. All answers must use already-seeded people.

High priority · 5 of 6

Define what survival means after the controlled landing

Why this improves play

The water ultimately corrodes a slowly landed city, so reaching higher tiers buys time rather than securing an ending. The blueprint promises control over falling, floating or landing but does not define how the latter two remain physically possible after the engines give out.

Source evidence · 3 excerpts
locations[4].description“anything that settles onto it slowly is corroded from below until it sinks”
locations[4].description“reach the highest tiers or evacuate the outer wards”
acts[2].turningPoint“whether the city falls, floats, or lands”
Recommended change

Choose and seed a real survival destination or a limited method of sustaining altitude. Make the last decision allocate time, lift and evacuation capacity, with visible consequences. Keep the leviathan’s disposition part of the same physical plan.

Suggested wording or scene · proposal, not existing canon

Proposed option to develop: emergency ballast can hold Cassivel above the sea for one evacuation window, but only if the core stays bound. A controlled descent reaches an established shelf above the corrosive water; its route must be discovered before the finale. If no such refuge exists in the intended setting, call the landing a temporary reprieve and resolve the next evacuation in the ending.

How to verify the improvement

Trace the survivors for the next twenty-four hours after each offered ending. There must be a reason they are still alive beyond “they climbed higher.”

Medium priority · 6 of 6

Give help limits without turning every kindness into another trap

Why this improves play

Every minor favor can generate another hidden obligation, an accumulating narrative burden with no limit or settlement rule. At the opposite extreme, Clank’s unlimited ability to find the needed object can dissolve equipment and escape problems. Both reduce the value of planning and earned trust.

Source evidence · 3 excerpts
rules[1]“Whenever an ally NPC (Redd Salter, Pell, or any other) aids the player”
rules[1]“must immediately and silently note a specific future cost”
npcs[6].notes“they're always capable of finding exactly what is needed in a given situation”
Recommended change

Reserve consequential debts for consequential help, state or foreshadow the price, and settle each debt once. Replace the stale Pell reference with the intended current character if retained. Give Clank a narrow salvage talent: he finds plausible nearby materials, not unique answers or essential evidence.

Suggested wording or scene · proposal, not existing canon

Proposed relationship rule: A faction’s substantial aid may carry one explicit or clearly foreshadowed obligation. Small kindnesses need not create debts. Once an obligation is fulfilled or refused with consequences, close it. Clank can locate mundane salvage within the established scene, but cannot produce unique keys, proof or a cure from nowhere.

How to verify the improvement

Track five acts of assistance through a paper session. The player should know which commitments remain, and Clank should help execute a plan without replacing it.

Keep the current pitch. It accurately establishes imposed debt, the vertical city, and faction conflict without spoiling the leviathan. No cosmetic rewrite is needed.

The first revision pass

First repair the cut-off fields and the forged-tag contradiction. Then settle Ferrin’s role and the physical ending. Test a refusal of Vessa before expanding faction errands.
