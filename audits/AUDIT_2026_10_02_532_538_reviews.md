## 1. What the fixes do

- **#532** A character without a sheet gets a voice pinned on the roster row at first speech. When the GM unmasks them with a merge, the survivor keeps the voice already heard: the merge fold fills the voice fields the survivor lacks, a name whose merge is still pending borrows the other name's voice on the reveal turn, and a voice of the other sex is not carried.
- **#539** A delivery direction or a speed set on a sheetless character's card follows them onto a sheet that is made or regenerated.
- **#538** The hero is never filed as an NPC. The player's exact name resolves to itself before anything else, a record named like the player is never a candidate for another name, and six tag handlers refuse the player's name before any write.

- The loan is the newest and least proven part. A pending merge hint does not only come from a GM tag: the summary's `sameNpc` list and the engine's own name-variant scan queue hints too (`_queueMergeHint`, memory.js).
