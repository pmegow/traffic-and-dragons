The two new axes

Definitions & the short-run standard

Narrative continuity
Does the run read as one continuous fiction? Measured by premise retention across the span (the caravan-mention scan), scene-to-scene causality on the story read, and the absence of loops, teleports, and dropped threads. Distinct from drift: a story can wander without ever contradicting state.
Drift avoidance
Does the prose stay in agreement with committed state — the axis the engine’s anti-drift stack exists to protect? Failures here are silent by nature: a sheet frozen while the story narrates wounds and loot, or prose defying an injected death. The owner’s standard applies: fifty turns is a shallow run, so any drift visible at this depth is a real problem, not a rounding error. By that standard all three budget-class arms fail outright, Gemini 3.7 Flash carries one self-resolving blemish, and the four clean sheets are all frontier-class.

Note on scope: drift avoidance graded here covers state/prose agreement observable in 50 turns. The long-horizon drift classes the engine guards against (memory-tier decay, alias fragmentation, canon erosion across hundreds of turns) are beyond a shallow run’s reach by construction — the mature-campaign evidence for those lives in the Runelords field record, not this study.
