1. Recommendation and scope

Replace named Village bindings with environment → sound palette → playback. The GM supplies a small description of conditions already established for the player. The engine validates and saves that metadata against canonical location identity. A deterministic director chooses recordings from an approved catalog. The GM never chooses a filename, URL, gain or playback command.

A newly discovered blacksmith in an adventure can receive forge ambience without a developer configuring that shop. An abandoned workshop cannot receive hammering simply because its function is “workshop.” A quiet room can remain quiet. Audio illustrates established conditions; it is never evidence of an actor, clue, attack result or rule.

First implementation after approval

General ambience: one stable environment bed and, where supported, one continuous feature such as fire or water. Automatic classification during ordinary scene establishment, consistent returns, smooth changes, saved preferences and conservative fallback.

Designed extensions

Ongoing work patterns, weather, action sounds and optional music use the same catalog and mixer. Each has its own release gate. A narrated sword swing is not assumed to be an engine-owned event.

Preserve the owner's current default: ambience ON on an unsaved device, explicit OFF and volume retained. Music starts OFF. Future action sounds require their own release decision. Normal play needs no sound authoring or per-location setup.

3. One direction of authority

  1. Established sceneAccepted game state; player-disclosed conditions
  2. Validated metadataPlace identity, enclosure and current evidence
  3. Audio planCompatible logical recordings and role gains
  4. Bounded playerLoad, fade, duck, suppress and release

The arrow never runs backwards. Muting audio, a failed download or an unavailable device cannot change inventory, clock, NPCs, location, quests, dice or mechanical outcomes. Cosmetic variation uses a separate deterministic seed, never gameplay randomness.

A new accepted-scene publication

The current UI refresh is not a commit boundary. applyMuts(...,{deferSave:true}) still calls syncUI(), which refreshes ambience. commitGmTurn() subsequently handles death/checkpoint consequences, history and persistence. Audio must not react to those intermediate states.

Propose one AudioSceneCommitted receipt after final location, mutation outcomes, death/restore consequences, metadata validation and local persistence outcome are settled. Only that boundary replaces the director's immutable scene snapshot. Volume, capture and pause update mix immediately without rereading a partially applied scene. Persistence failures remain visible under existing policy; no effect comes from an incomplete or rolled-back transaction.

Load, import, checkpoint restore, cloud adoption and character switch explicitly republish eligible continuous roles after evidence revalidation. A valid fire layer may resume; one-shots and scheduled accents never replay. Review calls, Table Talk, historical rendering, rerolls without mutations and generic sheet-sync calls cannot manufacture new scene evidence. This receipt is a proposed seam, not an existing API.

Standalone engine actions are producers too. Direct Rest advances the clock outside commitGmTurn(). After their own accepted save/consequence boundary, Rest, clock correction and approved identity repair must publish a fresh settled snapshot and invalidate affected evidence. They do not create new GM classifications. Otherwise the immutable snapshot could retain stale daytime or work audio until another GM turn.

Technical findings: applyMuts, syncUI, commitGmTurn / syncCharSheet. Exact insertion, persistence-failure and checkpoint semantics require Fable's protected-surface review; no duplicate parser in the UI adapter.

4. Semantic data and freshness

One validation registry defines enums, limits, provenance and repair rules. Names below are proposed. Static place characteristics, current evidence and device playback have different lifetimes.

RecordProposed contents
memory.map.nodes[key].soundscapeSchema; enclosure (open / covered / sealed / unspecified); setting; optional biome/function; at most four stable physical features; quiet/absence constraints; source and profileRevision. One record, ≤1 KiB.
worldState.audioSceneOne record: canonical node, visit/evidence revision, source turn, at most four currently audible activity facts and explicit absences. A hearth belongs to a place; a fire burning now is scene evidence.
Selection identityEngine-assigned immutable catalog cohort and stable variant key in the profile. Logical choices, not filenames or GM instructions.
Device preferencesEnabled/volume and later effects/music controls. Explicit OFF survives save imports, new defaults and resume.
Playback generationDevice-only cancellation generation plus sources, buffers, fades and pending work. Increment on replacement/disposal; never serialize handles or playing offsets.

Three revisions, not one: profile revision changes when durable classification changes; visit/evidence revision distinguishes leave-and-return; playback generation rejects obsolete asynchronous work even when campaign ID and turn are unchanged. The engine stamps them and provenance. A source turn substitutes for none of these.

Unknown, quiet and absent

unspecified means abstain or choose a provably compatible fallback. quietPolicy: silent is a complete classification suppressing decorative beds, incidental patterns and music, not a missing profile. hushed allows only quiet-compatible textures. A bounded forbid set excludes inseparable birds, voices or other content. Necessary fictional sounds remain in narration.

soundscape: {
  schema: 1, enclosure: "sealed", setting: "settlement",
  biome: "unspecified", function: "workshop",
  features: ["hearth"], quietPolicy: "normal",
  forbid: ["birds", "crowd"],
  provenance: { source: "gm-established", turn: 42 },
  profileRevision: 1,
  selection: { cohort: "core-nature-1", variantKey: "engine-assigned" }
}

Illustrative shape. A hearth alone does not authorize a fire loop. Keep the first vocabulary small and grounded in actual catalog coverage.

Freshness without fictional timers

The draft's blanket 15-minute activity expiry is removed. Evidence may persist through an ordinary stationary conversation; departure, explicit cessation, material location change, identity conflict, rest/montage, clock repair or a material time jump invalidate it. Import/reconciliation/restore revalidate evidence, not playing flags. Reloading the same save may retain valid evidence for the same visit and clock.

One activity-policy table defines validity and renewal. Use an authoritative end time when a real engine producer has one; otherwise Fable and the owner must settle what counts as a material time jump before activity rollout. Expiry withdraws permission to render a sound; it never extinguishes a fire or stops a worker. Fade that layer only. Reconfirmation rides the next ordinary turn, never a paid poll or loop timer.

Durable does not mean immutable. A roof collapse, removed forge or rebuilt tavern invalidates affected profile fields. Existing LOCATION_DESC is write-once; accepted material changes live in stateNotes. Profile correction/removal must follow those facts, never overrule them. Omission keeps valid fields, unspecified withdraws a classification, explicit absence forbids content, and accepted replacement atomically swaps the coherent record. Authored data wins stylistic choice, not a later established physical change.

5. How a new place receives sound

  1. Prefer authored data. Blueprint/location metadata seeds the same profile used everywhere else. Existing Village scenes migrate through a small authored seed table. Preserve its current fire-hours behavior as a documented compatibility rule until a reviewed activity producer replaces it.
  2. Classify during ordinary establishment. A proposed SOUNDSCAPE tag family supplies bounded place and current-scene descriptors with a normal GM response. No extra model call, name matching, prose keyword detector or per-loop request.
  3. Stage, then validate. Queue candidates in the real tag pipeline. After the turn settles, validate request campaign/generation and resolved intended target against the final accepted camera node. A refused move cannot put its destination's audio at the origin. Ambiguous targets and incompatible multiple candidates produce no new classification.
  4. Publish accepted metadata only. Whitelist enums, counts and sizes. Reject URLs, asset IDs, prompts, remote actors, hidden-state selectors, malformed values and stale responses. Omission preserves a valid profile and explicit absences. Missing metadata yields compatible neutral sound or silence, never player repair work.

Wire grammar is a Fable review item. The proposed payload has place and scene scopes, a resolvable intended target and typed fields. The engine assigns provenance, revisions, generation and selection identity. Use the shared tag strip registry; neither tags nor classification requests may become narrated content. A turn that leaves and returns to the same node still changes visit/evidence identity.

A missing-profile note has low priority within the existing three-builder/approximately 2,500-character budget: one request and at most one retry per visit. Register its builder, shape, latches, acknowledgment, deferral, failed-call and refusal restoration through the existing note system. It yields to consequence notes; explicit quiet is complete. Stable grammar enters the stable prompt once; only a compact current projection enters the volatile half. No repeated reclassification or playback histories in summaries.

Authority and its limit

The GM supplies conditions already established and perceivable to the player. Identity and enum checks cannot prove that description true. Mitigate the residual with restricted fields, public-evidence instructions, hidden-state invariance fixtures and an inspectable correction path. Hidden actors, hostility, future encounters and unrevealed blueprint metadata are ineligible—even if the GM sees them. A disclosed unidentified sound could be supported later without revealing its cause.

Overrides are optional authoring controls and cannot bypass content compatibility or voice gates. Correct an incorrect sound by correcting cosmetic metadata or suppressing it; never amend campaign canon to justify the recording.

6. Persistence and identity repair

A new property does not automatically survive repair. Current location merge folds selected fields; split constructs fresh nodes. Audio requires explicit repair rules and tests.

Proposed combined serialized audio budget: 256 KiB per campaign. Keep existing profiles at the limit; further places use transient/generic playback. Report capacity in diagnostics rather than evict authored intent or block a save. Validate the budget against large real saves; it is not a limit on the map itself.

7. Dynamic and predictable selection

  1. Apply device mute, capture, pause and lifecycle suppression.
  2. Apply explicit quiet/absence and perceptual compatibility constraints.
  3. Resolve a compatible authored preset, otherwise match canonical profile and valid current conditions.
  4. Filter role, listening approval, publication clearance, enclosure, time, biome and every inseparable content tag.
  5. Rank survivors by declared specificity; use saved cohort and stable variant key among equivalent choices. Stable logical ID is the final tie-break.
  6. When none fits, choose a demonstrably compatible neutral texture or silence. Never choose a random catalog item.

Catalog additions must not reroll established places. A seed and location cannot guarantee this if the candidate list grows. Freeze cohort membership and match rules for existing profiles; additions enter a new cohort. Retirement/replacement needs an explicit catalog migration. Logical IDs map to versioned files. An alias preserves the variant key; a merge/split chooses it under the repair rules.

Retain the same eligible source across unchanged plans and neighboring locations using the same asset/role. Do not restart at every UI update or paragraph. Campaign kind can seed authored data but is no longer a general audio permission gate.

Established conditionsExpected choice
New enclosed workshop; an audible burning fireCompatible room texture if available, plus fire. No hammer without supported working activity.
Abandoned workshop; no fire or workersNeutral compatible room bed or silence. “Workshop” alone cannot imply work.
Open square beneath a settlement nodeOutdoor clock-based bed. Child-node status is irrelevant to enclosure.
Empty coastal market at night; surf audibleCompatible night/coastal texture, no crowd just because it is a market.
Inland daytime market with an audible crowdCrowd-compatible daytime sound, no surf. Reject recordings with incompatible embedded content.
Quiet shrine without worshippers; birdless forestNo generic chanting or birds. Silence can be the complete correct result.

Weather gets one normalization boundary. A proposed typed annotation must be bound to the exact current weather text and accepted scene revision; changed, stale or ambiguous text becomes unspecified. General weather layering waits for that contract. “The rain has stopped” must not trigger rain through loose keyword matching.

8. Mixing and transitions

Initially two continuous roles: environment bed and optional feature. Each has a scene envelope independent of speech/capture gain. Crossfade only the changed role, retain unchanged sources, and preserve rapid-reversal reuse and generation-based cancellation.

Normal scene/time changeDefault three-second overlap when the destination is admitted and ready. Reuse an equal asset/role; release the oldest outgoing voice if rapid transitions reach the cap.

Narration / synthesis waitBegin with roughly 10 dB ducking as a tuning point, not a proven final mix. Suspend incidental work patterns. Narration never waits for sound loading.

Capture guarantee has a boundary. UI earcons and their reverb, and TTS acknowledgement/ready/failure sounds, are separate paths. Sharing Sound's context does not route them through the director. Quieting these too requires explicit dry/wet capture gates and a reviewed voice-control acknowledgement policy. Do not claim global silence or suspend the shared context as a shortcut.

State time is not spoken time

First-slice transitions follow final scene commit, which may precede the narrator saying “you step outside.” This is provisional and must be auditioned with a slow departure. If it sounds contradictory, the release gate fails and Fable must review a cancellable TTS presentation-boundary receipt. Do not guess word timing from raw prose or delay game-state commitment for sound.

A saved preference expresses intent; it cannot bypass browser autoplay policy.

9. Effects, work patterns and music

One-shots follow actual accepted events

Propose typed AudioEventReceipt producers: origin, campaign/timeline generation, accepted transaction ID, ordinal within that transaction, type, outcome, normalized material/action and perceptual eligibility. One registry maps receipts to approved variants. The deduplication key is campaign + timeline + origin + acceptedTransactionId + ordinalWithinTransaction; local-roll and GM producers each mint/reuse their real accepted transaction identity. It must distinguish successive turns’ first events. Event identity is engine-owned; no raw narration/tag keyword triggers.

Different origins have different commit points. A local player die roll is already resolved and saved before the GM call. Its sound can occur there even if narration later fails; retry must not replay it. GM-derived events publish only after their accepted transaction. A GM DICE tag or HP change alone cannot identify a blade, mace, swing, miss or impact. Add weapon sounds only when real typed outcomes and timing exist.

History, reload, replay, import and non-mutating rerolls emit no fresh effects. A 64-ID ring is secondary deduplication, not a guarantee against arbitrary old replay; producers must emit live accepted events once. New timeline generation separates genuinely new events after restoration. One effect voice, fixed priority and per-category cooldown; collisions/stale cues are dropped. During speech, drop decorative cues by default. A later explicit presentation marker may authorize an aligned quiet effect. No queued burst and no hit sound for a miss.

Ongoing work is a pattern

Working-smith audio requires fresh supported work evidence and a stop rule. Residence, presence or opening hours alone cannot authorize it. Use reviewed phrases of impacts and rests, a single bounded look-ahead schedule, and speech/capture suppression. Never install an interval per visit. This is later than the initial continuous ambience slice.

Music remains optional

Use an explicit author/player palette or broad disclosed setting; never hidden danger, villainy or future encounters. Music starts OFF, has its own control, and consumes an existing continuous slot. Long-track streaming versus short decoded loops needs its own buffering, transition and cache measurements. Do not decode albums into memory. No generated-music service or new account is required.

New content adds registry entries, not scattered conditionals. Browser manifests expose delivery metadata only; private purchase provenance, original WAV files and review exports remain outside public git.

11. Delivery and resource limits

Dynamic selection does not require new hosting. Keep the cleared starter catalog in same-origin sfx/. A later R2/CDN locator adapter changes delivery, not semantic matching. This proposal does not assert that a bucket or production media domain has been configured.

R2 delivery requires immutable versioned objects, allowed application origins, bounded requests and recoverable errors. Cloudflare documents custom-domain public delivery/cache integration and CORS configuration. CORS is not file secrecy or publication clearance. Moving purchased files out of git does not itself establish permission to serve them publicly.

ResourceProposed limit / overflow behavior
Director voicesTwo steady continuous roles; at most four continuous voices including outgoing tails, plus one short effect. Music later uses a continuous slot. TTS, capture and UI earcons are additional existing resources.
Decoded audio48 MiB managed-buffer admission budget including active buffers, cache references, tails and worst-case in-flight reservation. One load/decode slot. Reserve trusted maxima using actual context sample rate before decoding; validate result. Fade/release old audio first if overlap cannot fit.
Canceled decodeCancellation rejects its result, not its allocation. Keep the slot and reservation until native decode settles; then recheck generation and release stale results. Never start a second decode merely because the first timed out.
Encoded fetch8 MiB cap enforced while reading, not only after an unrestricted arrayBuffer allocation. Abort excess bytes even with missing/false Content-Length. Fifteen-second load timeout is a starting policy.
Device audio cacheSeparate versioned owner; proposed 32 MiB / 32 objects, LRU eviction, one serialized writer. No whole-library preload. Playback can continue without persistence when a cache write fails. Stop the existing generic SW route double-storing audio once this owner is installed.
Schedulers/logsOne pending transition per role; one bounded look-ahead per admitted pattern; 64 recent event IDs; 20 diagnostics. Cancel/dispose every handle.
Campaign recordsOne place record ≤1 KiB; one current-scene record ≤2 KiB; ≤256 KiB combined. At most four features/activities and eight content prohibitions. Capacity refusal affects sound only.

These are proposed budgets to measure, not a 48 MiB process-memory guarantee. Decoder intermediates, HTTP/SW allocation, TTS/Piper/WASM and capture remain additional. Test 48/96 kHz contexts and low-memory phones. Remove optional layers before sacrificing the main bed; never block a turn or reduce narration reliability to fit audio.

Existing network-first cache fallback is incidental. The proposed bounded cache can replay assets already present; uncached scenes remain unavailable offline. file:// retains its current unsupported-fetch status; localhost/hosted play is the acceptance surface. Offline packs, private distribution and long music caching require separate accepted designs.

12. Player controls and failures

Rename “Village ambience” to “Ambience.” Provide a direct mute reachable during play as well as Settings, with clear keyboard/screen-reader naming. Suggested copy: Ambience follows the scene. Your setting is saved on this device. Story flow never exposes tags, enum fields, filenames or classification diagnostics.

Preserve existing spoken pause/resume session intent. Propose deterministic typed/voice “ambience off” / “ambience on” commands with no GM call, clock advance or model interpretation. This is new routing for Fable review. It does not make the closed mic hear commands during narration; existing tap/control remains the interruption route then. Resume cannot override saved OFF.

Show concise playback status. An optional diagnostic view explains canonical node, evidence/quiet constraints, chosen preset, absent roles, capacity and actual failure. No compatible recording is normal silence, not an error popup. Download/decode/cache failure stays visible and attributable, deduplicated per attempt with explicit retry—not a toast on every sync. Gameplay remains available.

Keep authoring/library review in a separate focused tool. Optional overrides support world builders without turning ordinary play into administration. Sound cannot carry the only copy of required information.

14. What would count as working

Technical correctness and listening acceptance are separate gates. Persona review and a green test cannot establish that a recording or mix sounds good.

Actual listening before release

  • Speech first: soft speech, a long sentence, a quiet pause and intimate conversation. Did any word become harder to understand?
  • Honest scene: active/abandoned workshop, explicit quiet and contrasting markets. Did sound imply an unestablished person, creature, event or clue?
  • Attention: repeated loops and work phrases. Did repetition, rooster, click, level jump or gratuitous variation interrupt attention?
  • Timing: slow departure into a different acoustic space. If commit-time switching feels premature, presentation-boundary work becomes a launch prerequisite.
  • Outputs/session: headphones, phone speaker and parked-car audition; cold/missing assets and a short session with saved OFF. No claim of driving suitability or guaranteed background reliability.

Use full engine/standalone gates, test-first core assertions and named sabotage cases identifying the actual guarding tests. Browser fixtures must exercise failure inputs. An unrelated failing CI baseline gets its own reviewed workstream; never weaken it to land audio.

15. Expert panel review

Method: an independent AI reviewer applied all nine established panel lenses to draft A while a separate reviewer checked the actual architecture. These are rubrics of documented positions, not participation or endorsement by the named people. Every audio application below is reviewer inference. Grades retain the source files' meaning: ● primary, ◐ secondary. No audio was auditioned in this design review.

Gygax — world coherence without excess simulation

§7.1 ●, §7.3 ●, §1.1 ●. The clock/evidence direction fits the lens; a universal 15-minute lifetime could falsely imply a continuing fire stopped. Applied: remove that timer, distinguish evidence freshness from fiction, and test stationary scenes and time jumps. Car Mode experience is outside this lens's stated competence.

Harper — explicit authority and perceptible fiction

§3.5 ●, §7.8 ●, §7.4 ●. Current-node binding alone cannot establish perceptibility. Applied: distinguish GM descriptive input, engine acceptance and director selection; prohibit unrevealed metadata. Disclosed unidentified sound need not reveal its cause. This remains a grounding requirement, not a solved validator.

Laws — information flow and intended experience

§1.1 ●, §1.2 ●, §8.2–8.4 ●. Required information must survive muted play; mechanical tests alone do not demonstrate the experience. Applied: information-equivalence tests and three listening questions: misleading implication, speech clarity and distracting repetition. No player taxonomy was used to invent an audio preference.

Baker — graceful behavior when a filing is missing

§7.1 ●, §7.4 ●, §7.7 ●. Missing metadata should collapse to ordinary play. Unknown classification and established quiet need different handling. Applied: explicit quiet/absence, no repeated requests for a complete quiet profile, and separate missing/malformed/quiet tests. The player never repairs a tag.

Moran — protect quiet scenes

§5.1 ●. Pastoral pacing does not require a scene to escalate to count as play. Its application to acoustic quiet is an analogy. Applied: a long undramatic conversation/shared domestic activity becomes an acceptance scenario; no missing-profile request demands action, longer description or a filled sound field.

Leiber — particularity with restraint

§2.2 ●; §9.2 ●/◐. Specific sensory character need not mean every available effect. Applied: empty coastal night market versus occupied inland daytime market, driven only by established water/activity differences. The mixed-grade understatement evidence does not justify a mixer rule or horror sting.

Le Guin — room for the spoken language

§1.2 ●; §6.3 ◐. A fixed ducking number does not establish audible language or rhythm. Applied: actual narration, soft passages, long sentences and pauses in listening acceptance. The lens's §17 protocol requires corpus-measured prose findings; none were gathered here, so a prose-quality verdict is deferred.

Jemisin — no imported world assumptions or spoilers

§2.8 ●, §2.10 ●, §7.3 ●. Shrine/market/forest labels can smuggle unsupported culture, crowds or fauna into a recording. Applied: every inseparable contains component is a compatibility predicate; explicit content prohibitions and hidden-state invariance tests. A shrine never authorizes chanting by itself.

Abercrombie — telling detail and contrast

21. Proposal: the accent layer (occasional sounds within a place)

22 September 2026 · draft for owner review · nothing below is built or approved

A place keeps its one steady bed. On top of it, now and then, and only while nobody is speaking, it may add a short sound that belongs there: a wind chime, a few footsteps on a wooden floor, a bell far off.

Owner rulings this design starts from (2026-09-22). (1) A place gets its accents from its soundscape profile first. Sounds the GM calls for at a story moment are a later, separate project; they are the one-shot events of §9. (2) Accents never play over narration. They wait for the gaps between speech. Car Mode is the reason.

21.1 One layer model instead of a second special case

The player today knows one kind of sound: a looping bed, with a three-second overlap while places change. Weather, accents, events and music would each become a special case in createAmbientController if added one at a time. Instead, one table describes every layer kind, and the controller runs one rule set per entry. Adding music later means adding a row, not a new code path.

Accent and event share one short-sound voice, which keeps the §11 limit of "one short effect" intact.

21.2 An accent set is a catalog entry

Accents enter dev/audio-delivery.json and the generated audio-catalog.js like beds do, with role: "accent". They reuse every matching field a bed has (contents, enclosures, settings, biomes, clock band, cohort, approvals). Three fields are new:

  • sprite: all samples of one set packed into one file, with a cut list of start and end times. Five footsteps are one download, one checksum, one decode and one cache entry, not five. This matters because the loader has one decode slot and the device audio cache holds at most 32 objects.
  • pattern: either single (one strike: a chime, a bowl, a bell) or burst (a count range and a cadence range: 3 to 7 steps, 0.45 to 0.6 s apart, never the same step twice in a row). This answers the owner's note on the footsteps: "how many times this plays, or a range and cadence."
  • gap: the random wait range between two plays of this set, and trigger: ambient (the scheduler below may play it) or cued (catalogued now, played only when the §9 event project exists).

An accent's approval record drops the loop field and keeps recording, rights, contents and mix. "Mix" is only granted after the owner hears the set as the game would play it: the audition page gets a "play as in game" button that runs the real pattern and gap over the bed it will sit on.

21.3 How a place gets its accents

Selection works like the bed's: an accent set is eligible when the place's saved profile allows every sound it contains, matches its enclosure, setting, biome and clock band, and all approvals are true. A place takes at most two ambient sets, picked deterministically from its variant hash, so the same shrine always has the same chimes. Which sample plays within a set is random.

The gap is vocabulary. Today's content words are birds, insects, wind, water, fire, crowd, voices, rain, thunder, animals, machinery and music. None of them says "chimes" or "bells". Two routes:

  • Add words (chimes, bells) to AUDIO_CONTENTS and to the SOUNDSCAPE note that teaches the GM the list (api.js). That changes GM-facing text, so it is Fable-tier. Places classified before the change have no such words and stay without chimes until reclassified; the note's change-of-stamp rule does not reclassify them by itself.
  • Derive without new words: footsteps need voices or crowd allowed plus an interior setting (someone else is in the house, which also answers the "eerie when alone" worry); chimes and bells could only come from authored seeds (Village bindings, blueprint location metadata). Opus-legal, but no adventure shrine would ever chime.

Recommendation: ship footsteps by derivation first (no vocabulary change), and take the chimes/bells words to Fable as one small review.

21.4 The scheduler

One pure policy function decides, one driver timer carries it out. Policy: accentNext(state, now, rng) returns either wait until t or play set s, samples [..] at offsets [..]. It is pure so the engine suite can drive it with a fake clock and a seeded random source.

  • Arrival: nothing for the first 20 s in a place; the bed fades in first.
  • Gap: each set waits a random time from its gap range (footsteps 90–240 s, chimes 45–150 s, bell 240–600 s as starting values for listening). At most one accent in any 30 s across all sets. Never the same set twice in a row when two are eligible.
  • Quiet gate: an accent may start only when the narrator has been silent for at least 3 s. TTS stops briefly between sentences; the 3 s settle keeps a chime from landing in that breath. If the due time falls during speech, the accent is dropped and rescheduled after the next quiet window. Nothing queues, so there is never a burst of saved-up sounds.
  • Burst timing: all steps of a burst are scheduled on the AudioContext clock when it starts, so the cadence is sample-accurate. A burst lasts at most about 5 s. If narration starts during it, the remaining steps are cancelled and the sounding one fades in 0.15 s.
  • Hard stops: microphone capture, pause, hold, a hidden page, OFF, a place change and a campaign switch cancel the schedule at once, the same as the bed (§8). After capture or pause nothing is replayed; the gap timer starts over.
  • Typed play: with narration off there is no speech to wait for; accents run on their gaps alone.

21.5 Loading and memory

  • Order: the bed loads first. The accent sprite loads after the bed's decode settles, through the same loader, checksum and audio cache. The loader's single decode slot stays single; the controller waits for it instead of racing it.
  • Size: a sprite is at most 1 MiB encoded and 4 MiB decoded (20 s of 48 kHz mono float). The first sets are far smaller: five footsteps are under 3 s of audio.
  • Budget: accent buffers count toward the 48 MiB decoded budget. If a sprite does not fit, the accent layer stays silent for that place and the bed keeps playing ("remove optional layers before sacrificing the main bed", §11).
  • Lifetime: buffers live while the place is current and are released when it is left, so a long session cannot accumulate them (the monotonic-resources rule).
  • Cache: the service worker's audio cache recognises catalog asset URLs today by bed.url; it would also recognise sprite.url. Its version already derives from the catalog hash, so nothing new has to be bumped by hand.

21.6 Preparation

A new dev/prepare-accent-sprite.py, beside prepare-ambience-loop.py: mono, DC removal, trim silence, 10 ms fade in and out per sample, per-set level match with a peak ceiling, pack with a short silence between samples, MP3 with the gapless header, and a receipt with each cut's times. Longer chime takes are cut into several phrases by hand-listed times, so one 2-minute recording yields a handful of distinct strikes.
