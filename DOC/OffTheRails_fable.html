1 · The three symptoms, adjudicated

1a · "Daeris has come out of nowhere (should be back at the tavern)" — CONFIRMED, the core failure

The story: at t1443 Daeris stays behind at the Magnimar inn (node Magnimar|Inn - Top Floor Room, the unnamed "tavern" with innkeeper Durnah):

"I'll stay here," Daeris says, not happily. "Someone has to be the one who isn't standing next to the cursed tower when it decides it doesn't like us. If you're not back by dark, I'm coming, and I won't be polite about it."

She is then mentioned in zero GM responses for 19 consecutive turns (t1444–t1462). At t1463 she is suddenly physically present INSIDE the Spire's hidden stairwell — a space whose only known door Morwen sealed from outside at t1457 — with the GM hedging its own invention in the placing sentence:

"…past her shoulder you catch Daeris's shape framed faint against the door, Message must have carried further than you'd hoped, or she just knows the sound of you running."

No bridge exists — no travel, no arrival, no door-opening, no reunion beat. The records then converged on the error within 3 turns: she is stamped into the stairwell map node (npcs:["Frizwick","Daeris"]), her lastSeenAt moved from "Magnimar" to "Magnimar|The Spire — Hidden Stairwell", her knowledge array now holds BOTH "Stayed behind at the inn, warning she'll come after them…" AND "Cast Sacred Flame to stagger the Drowned Grafting…" side by side, and the t1466 chapter summary records her in the fight. The contradiction is now canon-grade on every memory tier.

The trigger sequence, honestly: the prompt pressure was constant (see §3C), but the dam broke immediately after the player's own t1461/t1462 phrasing — "Slowly back towards the wives", "the room the wives are in" — which a canon-faithful GM had no engine-side counterweight against, because the engine's own state agreed the wives were all present. The player then reinforced it ("Yell for Daeris…", "Wives, range attacks only"). Corroborating detail: her mood stamp sat unrefreshed from t1433 for 32 turns — the engine's own mood-age instrument was quietly reporting her offscreen the whole time.

1b · "Morwen has gone from inside to outside" — CONFIRMED as a one-entry dialogue wobble + records-vs-story limbo

Prose-wise Morwen is continuously outside: she walks to the Spire with Ammut (t1444), reads the wards, stays at the seam when Ammut and Frizwick descend (t1456), and seals the door from the street side (t1457). The inside-flavored moment is confined to her own t1457 dialogue — "Sealing it doesn't mean locking us in… doesn't wander in behind us" — pronouns that include herself in the party inside, two turns before t1459 states the truth plainly ("Thirty feet and a stone door between you and Morwen"). After t1459 she is dropped entirely — 8 turns, no exit beat, no line, absent from the "Wives, ranged only" combat and from the t1466 mood emissions.

The deeper problem is her record state: like Daeris, she has NO split record — the engine considers her present, the party panel shows her full vitals, and every prompt serves her sheet as an in-scene actor. She is one player phrase away from the same materialization Daeris got. Her lastSeenAt is still the world-grain "Magnimar"; the story's "outside the sealed seam-door" exists nowhere in the data.

1c · "Frizwick doesn't have her health displayed" — HER STORY IS CLEAN; the display sighting traces to the stale-record era

Frizwick's prose continuity t1420–t1467 is unbroken — every move narrated (roofline → drainpipe → flank → stairs). At t1467 she has no split record and full HP (52/52); the panel should show her vitals today. The explanation that fits every timestamp: the split-vitals display (v1.544, deployed 08-04 08:26) switched on while her ancient sea-cave split record (standing since ≈t1265) was still on the books — so between that deploy and your t1431 hand-heal landing, her card legitimately showed (split: Sandpoint Coast - Sea Cave) instead of HP. 3 · Root causes, ranked

#CauseStanding
AThe split lifecycle is one-way in practice, and stay-behinds are its blind spot. [PARTY_SPLIT:]'s own doc line frames splitting as "a party member strikes out on their OWN" — a stay-behind (the most common split in real play: "wait here", "watch the door", "stay at the inn") doesn't match that framing, and the GM essentially never writes it. There are NO teeth for a narrated stay-behind without a tag — this exact inverse was recorded on the #135 row on 08-06 ("recorded, not proposed") and this campaign demonstrated it within a day.Unguarded (the demonstrated hole)
BThe dies-at-birth bug ate any tag the GM DID write. On v1.543–v1.546 (this device, this whole window) a stay-behind split written before the party departs was auto-purged in the same response. Fixed as v1.550 (per-response grace) — deployed 08-06 21:49, never reached the device.Fixed, undeployed-on-device
CPhantom-presence pressure: with no record, the prompt actively asserts presence. Party sheets render the absent member's full kit as an in-scene actor every turn; the roster and scene manifest list them; nothing says "not currently in the scene." The GM's only counterweight is its own fading narrative memory — which summarization compresses away (Daeris's stay-behind survived only as one knowledge line and one chapter clause). Given enough turns, prompt-canon beats story memory; the t1461 player phrasing merely set the date.Systemic — the design gap behind both symptoms
DConvergence writes launder the error into canon. Once the GM narrated Daeris present, the mention-driven bookkeeping (map node stamp, lastSeenAt, mood/status writes, extractor knowledge, chapters) all recorded it — no tier asks "wasn't this character elsewhere?" The record system is faithful to narration, which is correct by design (the record never commands the narrative), but it means a single confabulated presence is permanent within ~3 turns.By design; mitigated only by preventing (A–C)
EOld contamination residue is still servable.
