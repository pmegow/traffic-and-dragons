01 / The working budget

One person’s time, including review and play.

Assumption: the stated hours are the total owner-led, AI-assisted working budget. Design, coding supervision, test investigation, independent-review coordination, writing, deployment and owner acceptance all consume it. There is no hidden second engineer, free weekend playtest or assumed agent speed multiplier.

Weeks run Thursday–Wednesday to respect #599’s October 8 reset. The calendar reserves 18 hours for planned work + 4 hours for rework. Saturday provides 6 uninterrupted hours; Sunday has 4 planned hours and 2 reserve; Wednesday’s 2 hours are reserve. Other weekdays carry 2 planned hours each.

First 8 weeks: 144 planned + 32 reserve = 176 h. That reaches the inventory/trade work, live account checks, held voice branch, identity boundary, epithets and store hardening, followed by ending work and lead triage; it does not reach release acceptance or networked play.

Beta: 306 planned + 68 reserve = 374 h over 17 weeks.
Full plan: 606 planned + 142 reserve = 748 h over 34 weeks. The final week has an additional 6 h of unused planned capacity included in that reserve total.

Reserve is for failed reviews, newly reproduced defects, hardware retests and scope reconciliation—not extra feature commitments. Holidays, vacation and unavailable weekends have not been deducted because none were supplied. Each lost 22 hours adds about one calendar week; Thanksgiving and year-end availability deserve an early check. Unattended test runtime can overlap other work, but test failures and result review cannot.
