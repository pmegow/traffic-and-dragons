02

Assumptions

  • Capacity. 2 hours each weekday and 6 hours each weekend day: 22 hours a week, 176 in eight weeks. Hours are owner-attended session hours in one lane. A parallel lane is a lever (section 10), not planned capacity.
  • Weeks run Thursday to Wednesday, aligned to the Fable reset (Thursdays about 19:00 Pacific). Each Thursday's 2 hours is the review: the /thursday ladder before the reset, then triage of what it found into the coming week.
  • Estimates use the Effort column times this project's observed overhead. For drift-surface rows: S ≈ 1.5 h, M ≈ 4–8 h, L ≈ 14–26 h, XL 25 h and up. The overhead is the critical pre-review, the failing test first, the sabotage proof, one independent review per batch and the owner's check on a pre-change save. Off-drift rows run about 1.3× their Effort. Owner-only rows count the owner's time.
  • Why the overhead is real: #504 (S) failed two independent reviews. #518 (S) took three builds and a failed verification. #437 (M) has been open since 24 September.
  • Fable budget: one drift-heavy item per Fable week. Astra counts as Fable tier (owner ruling, 4 October). When the budget runs out mid-batch, the drift policy's stop rule applies, and that week falls back to owner checks and off-Fable rows.
  • In flight today: another session holds uncommitted work on review lead (10) of #527 (a growth tag for a companion named only in a [SAY:] tag). The plan assumes it lands before week 1; it is what unblocks #437.
  • Scope: the Feature backlog, the Known issues and the Clear-for-Release rows. Long-term goals (L1–L8) and the Just-in-case rows wait on their own triggers and are excluded. One exception: #22b's cheap half (one trust-fence sentence plus length caps on imported fields) is recommended before outside testers arrive.
  • "Beta" means invited testers on the subscription path with manual entitlements (the #288 era). Already in place: the sign-in-first boot, the metered gateway, the beta menu tier (#289), beta-guide.html and the tester runbook.
  • Holidays are flagged, not removed: Thanksgiving is week 8's Thursday. Christmas and New Year fall in weeks 12–13, which carry owner-paced work.
