3. Root causes addressed

The work was driven by mechanisms reproduced from the latest save and transcript, not by generic drift examples.

Substring classification

WEIGHTY_REL_RE matched wed inside Owed. The t1666 change from wife/husband to “Owed a favor” was therefore misclassified as weighty-to-weighty: no downgrade record, no bond warning, and a false permanent defining moment.

Unbounded language recognition

Phase, presence, SAY coverage, and consumable checks were using proxies that lost syntax, ownership, or playback structure. Those shortcuts created both missed incidents and false alarms.

Unobserved lifecycle gaps

Jorgenfist could remain unfiled, travel could arrive underpriced in clock time, and dated commitments or completed future events could remain unresolved because no bounded observer owned those transitions.

Canon ratchet after identity fusion

The old scholar was first identified as not Mokmurian, then silently resolved to Mokmurian. Summary extraction, NPC death state, chapter memory, quest completion, and downstream prose reinforced the unsupported identity.

5. W2 - referential integrity and atomic consequences

W2 fixes the mechanism that turned an anonymous scholar into Mokmurian: a durable NPC name is no longer accepted as proof that the named NPC occupies the physical scene role. The engine now persists the observed actor separately from the canonical identity and requires earlier committed evidence before irreversible identity writes.

Scene evidence

  • [SCENE_REF:handle|name or ?] records an observed actor; [SCENE_NOT:handle|name|explicit] or [SCENE_NOT:handle|name|inference] records polarity without treating uncertain POV as a hard negative.
  • [SCENE_REVEAL:handle|name] can resolve an anonymous actor, but its own response cannot authorize death. It also cannot overwrite a different established binding.
  • The active frame survives summaries. Location/sub-location transition seals the old frame until structured extraction succeeds, so the t1638 evidence survives the t1644 compaction boundary.
  • Actor, negative, and sealed-frame caps preserve accepted evidence. Overflow refuses new evidence and all high-impact writes fail closed until a structured summary covers the preserved transitioned frame.
  • Direct named death and combat-close propagation consult the same prior-turn evidence. A same-response scene tag cannot self-authorize a combat corpse.

Atomic transaction envelope

[CANON_TXN_BEGIN:stable-id|npc-death|canonical-name-or--|scene-handle|quest-title-or--]
[SCENE_DEATH:scene-handle] [NPC:canonical-name|dead|relation]
[QUEST_STEP:quest|objective|true] [XP:amount] [GOLD:+amount] [ITEM_GAINED:item]
[CANON_TXN_END:stable-id]

The response is preflighted before handlers run. Subject, handle, quest, and allowed operation types must agree. Valid operations run against cloned world and memory state while success messages, toasts, sounds, and level-up UI are buffered. State, receipt, and UI commit only after every handler succeeds; otherwise every enclosed effect rolls back and the claim is quarantined loudly. Independent envelopes still commit independently.

Semantic operation receipts preserve intentional repeated grants while making exact replay, 100 versus +100, duplicate envelopes in one response, and delayed continuations idempotent. A quarantined ID remains poisoned and keeps its original failure reason.

Merge and summary gates

  • Direct NPC merges no longer execute merely because a tag names two records. A typed provisional or the exact pair delivered through the existing merge-confirmation note must authorize it.
  • Summary npcDeaths entries now cite {name, handle, sourceTurn, canonTxnId?}. Bare names cannot mint a corpse once W2 is active.
  • The whole extraction validates before any tier writes. A contradicted death cannot partially file a chapter, lore, decision, NPC fact, or death stamp.
  • After three referential validation failures, a bounded diagnostic excerpt goes to memory.archive.identityQuarantines; the rejected claim is not promoted into chapter/event-history canon.
Exact t1667 replay. dev/replay-w2-incident.js loaded the 3.96 MB export (testRuns/Rise_of_the_Runelords__Ammut__Ammut_t1667.tnd), verified the t1638/t1647/t1648 scholar evidence, applied the already-ratified correction only to the in-memory clone, and replayed the old wrong-corpse bundle twice. 6. W3 - four recognizer repairs

Phase consistency

  • A closing quotation mark remains attached to the sentence it closes, while following narration is evaluated separately.
  • The word before rejects a cue only when it governs that phase cue; “Dawn breaks before you” remains valid.
  • Quoted phase mentions and whole-entry quote-parity distrust remain rejected.
  • The exact t1605 dusk/clock mismatch is exercised.

Party presence

  • Detection is subject-bound rather than keyword-bound.
  • It rejects possessives, co-location, commands, plans, counterfactuals, and a companion belonging to some other subject.
  • It accepts a named departure/send-ahead and an unambiguous adjacent pronoun.
  • Explicit out-of-character correction is separate; an in-story claim that a guard lies cannot activate it.

SAY playback coverage

  • Compliance follows the same segmentation and coverage boundaries used by actual playback.
  • A valid response with six SAY tags and twelve quotation marks is accepted.
  • Dialogue before the first tag and a new dialogue paragraph without a leading tag fail deterministically.

Consumables

  • Item canon/type lookup is authoritative; a generic head noun such as “fire” is not sufficient evidence.
  • Reusable classification explicitly overrides lexical suspicion.
  • Type-defined consumables outside the legacy name regex are detected.
  • Loss/kept suppression is scoped to the correct owner.

7. W4 - bounded observers for missing axes

These observers share one policy: the engine may identify strong evidence and prepare a specific note, but the GM remains the writer of canon. They are one-shot or bounded, combat-aware, use the shared note-builder registry, and never silently mutate the story fact they are watching.

ObserverImplemented behaviorFalse-positive boundary
Location filing A high-confidence enclosed-place entry may survive up to eight untagged turns before a note offers the location/sub-location fork. The exact Jorgenfist “through the breached curtain wall” case is covered. Rejects travel toward, map references, remembering, and the already-current place. LOCATION/SUBLOCATION suppress; descriptive tags alone do not.
Travel pricing Tracks one bounded journey, expected duration, and accumulated clock charge. At arrival it asks only for the computed shortfall. Never recharges the entire expected duration; an honest full time advance suppresses the note.
Dated commitment Money/obligation plus an interval can prompt the GM to choose SCHEDULE, FUTURE_EVENT, or QUEST. An existing lifecycle tag suppresses the prompt.
Strict future duration Durations such as “five days” and “in 2 days” go directly to the clock scheduler. Legacy strict-duration future events promote during the sweep. Fuzzy dates remain ordinary future events.
Resolution assist Token overlap plus an outcome verb in a fresh summary proposes a prefilled FUTURE_EVENT_RESOLVED tag. Mere mention, discussion, or a plan does not resolve anything; the engine does not auto-resolve.
Location twin An identity-layer conflict can queue a bounded note that offers SUBLOCATION as the likely repair. An already-live world node remains legal; refusal does not mutate the graph.

8. W5 - state integrity and provenance

Typed bookkeeping transcript rows

A pure-tag or clean-empty GM turn now retains a truthful, typed receipt with clock, tag, and version data while leaving story text empty. These rows survive serialization but are excluded from story UI, retrieval, summarization prose, voice, dialogue, and actions. This preserves provenance without feeding bookkeeping back into narrative.

Tag and mutation provenance

The tag log records legal two-letter HP/XP tags, and relationship descriptor changes receive meaningful non-empty mutation labels. This makes later forensic reconstruction possible without inventing story prose.

Player/NPC identity boundary

The player name and aliases are rejected by summary npcUpdates and by the NPC tag writer, and hidden from memory table-of-contents, detail, and retrieval name indexes. Companions remain intentionally represented in NPC memory; a blanket companion exclusion was not adopted.

Duplicate acquisition warning

Owner-scoped, exact-base-name acquisition can warn about an apparent duplicate but never blocks a tag. A paired loss suppresses the warning. Repeated grants remain legal because they may represent a documented quantity.

World/sub-location twin refusal

The identity layer refuses an attempt to create a world location that conflicts with an existing sub-location identity. The refusal creates no world node or edge, and the follow-up note offers the correct sub-location form.

Single authority for displayed time

The clock is the authority. Prompt and player-facing displays derive from it through a shared projection; stored world.time remains compatibility/flavor data only. Opening, skeleton, Table Talk, panels, error reporting, and sync wording were brought into that rule.

Mood freshness

Roster mood is injected only while fresh (at most twelve turns old). Stale or legacy-unstamped mood is withheld, while identity, relationship, and pronoun data remain available.

Persistent consumable follow-up

Pending consumable questions are keyed by owner and item, have a cooldown, deliver at most three times, and are capped at six stored records. They do not interrupt combat and clear on the appropriate player or companion kept/lost tag.

9. W6 completion — atomic summary identity validation

W6 is implemented in release v1.601. The root cause was a trust-boundary omission: extractor prose was treated as harmless compression even though it writes directly into chapters, event history, NPC details, lore, decisions, future events, and eventually eras. The t1644 extractor therefore turned male Ammut into she/her and injected the contradiction every turn.

Bounded explicit authority

Each extractor and era-compression prompt receives a per-call canonical identity table capped at 12 rows and 1,600 characters. Priority is player, sheeted party, canonically bound scene actors, then on-file NPCs actually mentioned in the source window. Player and party authority comes from sheet gender; ordinary NPCs require an explicit stored he/him, she/her, or they/them. The roster's synthetic fallback is excluded. Aliases are capped and resolve through the existing identity layer.

Conservative prewrite validation

One preflight runs before the first extraction write and inspects every prose-bearing output tier. It rejects only a high-confidence binary contradiction: a sole canonical actor named at the start of one sentence, followed in the adjacent unquoted sentence by subject/reflexive he/himself or she/herself from the opposite family. The exact t1644 wording fails at its second sentence. Separately named Morwen passes.

  • Mixed actors, quotations or broken quote parity, object/possessive pronouns, they, unknown/conflicting NPC pronouns, and non-adjacent references abstain.
  • The validator never replaces words or manufactures confidence; the whole generated extraction retries.
  • Era compile and merge use the same bounded prompt and prewrite check, preventing later compression from re-ratcheting the same identity error.

Failure lifecycle

The retry strike is now a single persisted worldState.summaryFailure record, saved immediately after each failure and restored on load. Attempts one and two retain the complete unextracted window. At attempt three, identity-validation failures retain the live tail and archive a capped, non-injected forensic receipt with the reason, subject, attempt count, and bounded raw source evidence. They file no generated chapter, event-history entry, or canon consequence. The receipt array is capped at 12, initialized/healed, and preserved by .tnd import.

10. W7 completion — structural relationship drift prevention

W7 shipped in v1.601. It was built as the internal v1.600 stage, but W2, W7 and W6 landed together in the single release commit c8c37f1, so v1.601 is the only marker that resolves to a tree. Its root cause was not merely a weak word list: one free-text relationship slot was being asked to represent both durable canon and a passing scene posture. Any last-write-wins update could therefore replace a marriage with a favor, and downstream readers would treat the replacement as roster authority, graph truth, reciprocity input, and a permanent defining moment.

One directed edge, two independent axes

Each relationship row is now {entity,bond,bondTurn,dynamic,dynamicTurn}. bond owns marriage, kinship, oath, alliance, enmity, and other standing canon. dynamic owns the current warmth, tension, suspicion, obligation, or favor. Wife/Husband directions remain independently authored; no reciprocal row is invented.

  • Only bond feeds core-memory detection, roster authority, the NPC graph, reciprocity, and the periodic durable-bond audit.
  • Dynamic remains separately labeled in prompt and sheets, but can never mint a relationship defining moment.
  • npc.rel remains an NPC stance and NPC_LINK remains graph data; neither seeds a character-sheet bond.

Write and removal authority

New explicit bond and dynamic tags own the two axes. A new bond may commit directly. Replacing or removing an existing bond—and explicitly removing an entire pair—stages the original value and requires the exact same operation on a later GM response. A same-response duplicate cannot confirm itself. Whole-pair receipts preserve both the prior bond and prior dynamic. Axis-specific removal cannot disturb the other axis.

Legacy relationship tags remain accepted only as a compatibility input. They do not guess an axis and do not mutate canon; instead they create a bounded, deduplicated, persistent explicit-axis decision. Multiple conflicting candidates on one edge survive independently, and resolving one cannot erase its siblings. Absent legacy removals have an explicit whole-pair disposition, so the queue cannot deadlock.

Lossless migration and identity convergence

Every legacy descriptor migrates verbatim to bond with its original turn and a persistent axis-review marker. No regex classifies it. If a hybrid or alias collision cannot enter a full eight-item decision queue, its source descriptor/row remains intact and retries when capacity opens. Existing long values are preserved; prospective writes and proposals over 240 characters are refused loudly without creating empty state residue.

The central adapter covers the player, companion sheets, generated sheets, character-library imports, read-only character inspection, PC swaps, NPC aliases, NPC merges, campaign loads, and prompt/UI readers. Portable inspection uses the inspected character's raw identity table and cannot mutate the active campaign. Campaign load installs the incoming memory alias table before migrating relationships, preventing the previous campaign from rekeying the new one. Merge logic preserves outgoing rows from both populated companion sheets, rekeys queued owners as well as entities, recomputes each record's own key, and leaves unrelated decisions unchanged. Player names and epithets resolve to one pair.

W1 protection retained

Legacy W1 downgrade records remain a protective preimage. Writing an unrelated dynamic first restores the saved bond. Re-emitting the already-clobbered text as a bond cannot exploit the row's current value: the protected bond is restored first, then the destructive change enters the normal two-response confirmation path. After three pointed notes, a guard becomes muted rather than deleted and remains visibly unresolved in the prompt. A correctly confirmed explicit bond change carries a receipt that prevents the old post-pass from re-arming a stale downgrade warning.

Exact incident outcome

The t1666 shape is now represented as two facts on each directed edge: the Wife bond survives, while “Owed a favor … warming” lands only on dynamic. The dynamic update creates no defining moment. This makes the original corruption structurally impossible even if a recognizer later misses a word.

11. Mokmurian canon repair

The story ruling supplied by the owner is: the party's identification was wrong; the old scholar was a deception; the real Mokmurian is alive and remains in Jorgenfist. The implementation turns that ruling into a narrow, receipted repair instead of directly editing old transcript or chapter prose.

Operator-only repair tag

[NPC_DEATH_RETRACTED:name|reason|existing remote node]

The tag is deliberately absent from the GM's normal prompt documentation. On a first repair it accepts only when the NPC already exists in world and memory, is recorded dead, has a non-empty reason, names an existing map node, and that node is remote from the party.

What the transaction changes

  • Archives the full changed preimage: world death/status/status-turn, memory death/last-seen/attitude/knowledge/events, and affected map membership.
  • Clears world and memory death stamps, death status, death-like attitude, and narrowly self-death-shaped facts.
  • Preserves unrelated facts. Tests retain “Killed three challengers” and “Remains inside Jorgenfist,” while removing facts such as “Slain by ...” and “Body posed ...”.
  • Files correction knowledge and durable lore, records one decision receipt, and moves Mokmurian's membership/last-seen to the already-existing Jorgenfist node.
  • Leaves awarded XP untouched. The repair is canon correction, not an automatic reward clawback.

The repair is paired with the quest reopening:

[QUEST_STEP:The Giants of Jorgenfist|Strike Mokmurian directly in his ritual chamber before his army can march|false]

Compatibility with the correction already pasted

The owner had already fed the earlier correction text to the GM before the final hardening pass. v1.598 therefore includes a completion replay, but it is not a general idempotent override. It accepts only a matching earlier correction receipt, unchanged repaired-node membership, and real residual death memory or missing correction knowledge. It archives that cleanup preimage and avoids duplicating the decision or reward.

A clean repeat is refused and cannot grow the archive. If the NPC has moved since the repair, the completion replay is also refused and cannot teleport him back to Jorgenfist.

Exact old-handler replay receipt. The simulated t1667 state ends with Mokmurian alive, false death attitude/facts/events removed, corrected knowledge present, XP unchanged, and decision count unchanged. A clean repeat produces no new archive entry.

12. Drift protection preserved

The hardening was checked against the anti-drift invariants throughout implementation.

  • New observers nudge a specific GM decision; they do not silently declare location, time, commitment, outcome, or consumption canon.
  • Identity refusals are mutation-free on failure.
  • Operator repair is hidden, gated by recorded state, archives its preimage, and is replay-safe.
  • Owner-scoped records prevent one character's item evidence from clearing another's.
  • Clock authority is centralized rather than copied into a second mutable truth.
  • Typed bookkeeping remains available for audit but cannot contaminate narrative retrieval or playback.
  • Existing legal tag behavior, repeated quantities, companions in memory, and reusable items remain supported.
  • No historical transcript, chapter, event history, or live save was rewritten in place.

Deliberate non-goal after W1-W7

All seven approved workstreams are implemented. One adjacent proposal remains deliberately outside their shipped scope:

  • Recurring-name registration: W6 validates existing explicit identity but does not infer or auto-register a new principal from repeated proper nouns. That remains proposal-first because it has different evidence and identity-fusion risks.

Shortcuts explicitly rejected

  • Blanket rewrite of transcript locations or the proposed sweep across dozens of historical rows.
  • Unreceipted direct edits or deletes in memory/state.
  • An ordinary offstage NPC tag as a substitute for a proper Sable correction.
  • Blanket companion exclusion from NPC memory.
  • Automatic aliasing based only on a given name.
  • A delayed RETCON guess or owner-blind companion XP heuristic.
  • Undocumented quantity/rename tag vocabulary.
  • Automatic XP/reward clawback for a canon correction.
  • Raw in-place chapter or event-history surgery.

The initial default branch for Mokmurian was also rejected pending owner intent. The owner then explicitly selected the deception/real-Mokmurian-alive branch, which is the branch implemented above.
