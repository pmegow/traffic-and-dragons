## Record

**Built as ruled.** The scene (`dice_roll.py`) reads the bake: a hit is the die's lowest point arrested within `HIT_Z` of the felt, ONE per landing at its peak fall speed (the first cut counted a landing twice across two frames); a rolling stretch is three or more frames on the felt still turning, recorded with its spin at both ends so the clatter slows as the die does; `--measure` prints the result and skips the render. `dice-clips.js --measure` re-bakes every shipped seed (sixty in 29 s, four at a time), refuses a seed whose rest box no longer matches its clip, and writes `hits`/`roll` plus `measured` on the manifest (and `dice/manifest.js`, now loaded by the game as `DICE_MANIFEST`). The record over the sixty: one to five hits a clip, landings up to 7.1 units/s at ~0.46–0.5 s, 76 rolling stretches. `dice-sound.js` turns a clip's record into a sound.js recipe (tick per hit by the energy's root of the speed, thud under a hard one, taps spaced by the die's tip angle over its spin, a rumble the stretch long, brighter for the small dice); `Sound.play` takes a recipe object; ui-dice.js starts the sound inside each clip's `play()` promise with the tile's die. Tests: the #659 section (3: the record, the recipe, the player and wiring); `dev/sabotage-659-dice-sound.js` (14 clauses). **Second cut (v1.1313, owner 2026-10-23: "just a tick" — Audio/Inspiration/dice.mp3 as the reference):** the reference measured (dev scratch, 5 ms RMS frames + Goertzel bands): a landing at -21 dBFS with its energy at 2 and 2.8 kHz over a 600-1000 Hz knock and a 100 Hz thump, ~30 ms decay, then five to seven bounces 25-55 ms apart whose tails ring 170-300 ms; the first cut was a 15 ms band-passed tick 17 dB quieter. A contact is now a CLACK — the burst in the die's band plus five decaying inharmonic partials (2000, 2800, 640, 1050, 3300 Hz × the die's body factor) — a hard landing adds a soft thump and a SETTLE cascade of smaller clacks (30-90 ms gaps, softer each, the surface ringing 0.4 s under the last two), roll taps are lighter clacks with a deterministic jitter, every note carries a `role`. Rendered through the real sound.js graph offline (OfflineAudioContext, the first play of a fresh page) the d6 landing now peaks at -12 dBFS with the 2 kHz band within 2 dB of the 800 Hz knock and the 100 Hz thump 2 dB under — the reference's shape. Found on the way: the roll record's starting spin was the landing frame's orientation jump (136 rad/s — forty taps a second); the scene wraps the quaternion angle to π and reads the median of the first three frames, the sixty clips were re-measured (max spin now 15 rad/s), and the record test refuses a spin over 40.
