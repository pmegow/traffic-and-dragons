Drift Hardening — reconciled action plan

Filed 2026-08-11 · engine v1.595 · reconciles driftHardening_fable.html (the #168 five-lane audit, 32 findings) with driftHardening_SOL.html (Sol's five-agent adjudication, 9 findings) · every point where the two reports disagree was re-adjudicated by fresh execution against the real save (Rise_of_the_Runelords__Ammut__Ammut_t1667.tnd) and v1.595 source before this plan was written — the probes and their raw output are recorded in §1

0. Executive summary

The two audits agree on the diagnosis: the anti-drift plumbing is sound, and what rotted is everything the plumbing never sees — recognizers that misread prose, axes with no detector, records that predate the guards, and receipts that land wrong. They overlap exactly on the week's flagship incident (the unanchored WEIGHTY_REL_RE matching "wed" inside "Owed a favor", so the bond-downgrade guard never armed and a false ★ defining moment landed on all four sheets), on the Jorgenfist location blackout, on the frozen world.time, and on the stay-behind detector's foot-on-calf false positive.

Where they diverge, each report caught something the other missed — and each got one thing wrong that the other corrects:

  • Sol's biggest catch, missed by the Fable audit: the wrong corpse. The t1648 kill prose kills "the old scholar-giant" — an actor the party's own committed dialogue had twice judged "not the warlord" ("Not Mokmurian himself, you think… an old scholar-giant rather than a war-king", t1638) — yet the chapter extractor canonized "Mokmurian dropped dead across his own dimming tablets", both stores stamp dead:1648, and the quest objective auto-completed with rewards. The Fable audit's clean list had cleared "death propagation works" — true of the plumbing, but nobody checked the stamp's referent. Confirmed by fresh transcript execution (§1.1).
  • Fable's biggest catches, missed or wrongly downgraded by Sol: the #158 phase detector's orphaned-quote blindness; the Collector's missing t414 death stamp and corpse/successor fusion; the Ilvane/Grafter alive-and-dead dual record; the travel-time compression axis; the t1606 transcript hole; and Sable — Sol's adjudication rejected "Sable drift", but fresh execution confirms the Fable finding exactly: 29 transcript scenes name her, zero retrieval-index entries carry her, and every line of her canon has been evicted to memory.archive.*, which is never injected (§1.3).
  • One Fable claim is refuted: the "schedule anchors don't ride clock repairs — three days is silently 3.81" finding fails on the receipt arithmetic. The Collector entry's born:6915 lies outside the corrupted clock interval, the repair receipt restored truth, and dueMin−born is exactly 3 days. Sol's closure of the clock incident was correct — and the proposed auto-rebase fix would have corrupted this very entry (§1.5).
  • Two Sol-only code findings are confirmed live and neither is in the Fable report: the SAY compliance estimator false-fires on every one of the five retained responses despite 4–7 [SAY:] tags each (§1.6), and the tag-provenance ring's name regex cannot record legal two-letter tags — 13 HP/XP mutations sit in the current ring with no recordable name (§1.7).
The plan in one paragraph. Close the Owed hole first (small diff, active corruption, both audits' #1). Kill the every-turn false SAY nudge and fix the other three broken recognizers under a new standing rule — recognizer fixtures beside sabotage. Add detectors to the three axes that have none (location filing, travel-day pricing, dated commitments). Land the small receipt fixes. Run ONE consolidated repair session on the live campaign — after the recognizer fixes, so repaired state isn't immediately re-rotted — with the owner adjudicating the forks (Mokmurian above all). In parallel, draft the one genuinely new subsystem both audits point at from different directions: referential integrity for high-impact writes (Sol's scene-entity gate), which is the only structural answer to the wrong-corpse class. That one is proposal-first, like the identity layer was.

1.5 The schedule-anchor claim fable refuted — Sol's clock closure was correct

schedule entry: born 6915 · dueMin 11235 · dueMin − born = 4320 = exactly 3 days
clock.repairs:  [{id:"142-double-decrement", before:6690, after:7850, t:1550}]
the regression (Sol §10 / the t1549 audit): 8820 → 6500 = the −1160 manual repair applied twice

The corrupted reading interval was [6500 … 6690] (then the receipt jumped to 7850, and readings resume true). A born of 6915 cannot have been stamped inside that window or after the repair — it is a pre-corruption, true stamp. The +1160 receipt restored the clock to truth (one of the two −1160 applications was legitimate), so the entry needs no rebase and "three days" is exactly three days. The Fable audit's "silently 3.81" assumed the anchors were stranded by the repair; the arithmetic says otherwise. Consequence for the plan: the proposed fix ("clockRepair shifts dependents' absolutes by the same delta") is dropped — it would have corrupted this very entry, because a truth-restoring repair must not move truthfully-stamped anchors. What survives is a design note: repair receipts now record before/after/t, which is enough for a future repair tool to adjudicate per-entry whether an anchor was born inside a corrupted window. No auto-rebase, ever.

7. Process rules this reconciliation adopts

  • Recognizer fixtures beside sabotage (the Fable audit's rule, ratified here): every guard that begins with a recognizer — regex, splitter, window-match, ratio estimator — ships with a fixture file of real campaign prose covering the hostile cases (dialogue-adjacent narration, possessives, substring traps, orphaned quotes, interrupted quote spans), and the failing-first test runs the recognizer over them, not a hand-seeded queue. Sabotage proves the plumbing; fixtures prove the eyes. Sol's SAY finding retroactively validates the rule — a fifth recognizer with the same untested-half failure.
  • The referent lane (this reconciliation's own lesson): an audit that verifies a stamp landed must also verify the stamp's referent. "Mokmurian is stamped dead in both stores and never acts again" was true and still wrong. Future audits add a referential-truth pass on identity-bearing writes: does the committed prose actually support the name on the record?
  • Boundedness requirements for new guards (Sol's, adopted): scene ledgers capped and scene-scoped; one bond + one dynamic per relationship pair; presence records as small rings, never per-turn appends; caps overflow loudly with the actor named; an unresolved integrity flag may stop nagging after its delivery budget but must stay visibly unresolved, never silently deleted.
  • The negative-results ledger: conclusions both audits reached that must not be re-litigated — the t1658 auto-rejoin is fixed by #164 (kept only as regression evidence); the clock double-decrement is closed with its receipt and the anchors are sound (§1.5); "RAG forgot the not-Mokmurian line" is false (retrievable); the player did not declare the scholar's identity (the GM supplied the mapping); quest lifecycle is not globally broken; party personality has not collapsed; the false engine notes are confirmed noise but not proven causes of the bond/identity failures; the t1667 空气 token is an isolated generation blemish.
