## Task

**Narration stuck in THIRD person — the exit correction was time-boxed, and single-player had no person directive at the end of the prompt at all.** TLDR: the GM is supposed to write vivid second person; the owner's live campaign was narrating the hero by name instead. Two independent causes, both confirmed by execution against the owner's own save. **RC-A (proven, the latent defect):** leaving hot-seat multiplayer sets `worldState.mpEnded`, which drives the D12 second-person reinforcement — and `commitGmTurn` retired it on a TURN COUNTER, three turns after the demote, whether or not the GM had ever switched back. The proof is in the transcript rather than the state (a snapshot cannot see turn 816): hot-seat rounds render a `Name:` prefix per player, and scanning `worldState.transcript` for player entries with ≥2 such segments gives t809–t816 (Ammut+Morwen) and t915 (Ammut+Frizwick). Overlaid on the per-response second-person count: multiplayer ran t809–816, third person correctly began at t813, the exit note fired t817–819, the GM did not comply, the counter cleared `mpEnded`, and the campaign then narrated Ammut in the third person for TEN MORE TURNS (t820–829) with no correction left anywhere, recovering at t830 only by luck. The corpus even contains the controlled A/B: at t915 the GM complied on the first note and the drift lasted ONE turn. Complied → 1 bad turn; missed the window → 13. **RC-B (structural, and the likelier cause of the report itself):** in ordinary single-player the post-STYLE slot was the EMPTY STRING, so the only second-person instruction in the whole prompt sat in the CACHED STABLE half ~100k characters earlier — while the prose voice occupied the end-of-prompt authority position this file documents as load-bearing. Measured across 10,067 real GM responses: campaigns running the **`howard`** voice are 2.7–5.3% second person (third person from their THIRD response, before any momentum exists) against 98–100% for `abercrombie` — and the owner's campaign `proseAuthor` is `howard` in the t1667 export (it was `muir`, then `wells`, earlier). A voice that narrates in third person by nature had nothing at the authority end of the prompt to answer to.

## Status / record

✅ **SHIPPED v1.605 (2026-08-12).** ① **Retirement is COMPLIANCE-boxed, never time-boxed** — `personDriftDetect` (api.js) clears `mpEnded` the moment a response actually narrates in second person, so a GM that complies retires it a turn EARLIER than the old counter and one that does not keeps being asked (the #167 lesson). ② **The empty slot is filled**: single-player now always ends with a short `PERSON: second person — 'you' is <name>` line, deliberately terse so it holds the person without competing with the prose voice. ③ **A cause-agnostic drift detector**, because the corpus shows drift that no multiplayer explains: `PERSON_DRIFT_MIN`=2 consecutive responses that narrate the hero by name with no second person outside dialogue arm `buildPersonDriftNudge` on the ENGINE-NOTE channel (the D12 round-3 channel — it rides the user message, the newest tokens, after the third-person prose it must overrule) plus the post-STYLE slot; one compliant response silences both. **Predicate measured, not guessed:** 10,043 judged responses, and the flagged set has a **ZERO** false-positive rate — the load-bearing clause is requiring the hero's name in the NARRATION, which suppresses exactly the 59-of-172 class that is pure dialogue, combat readouts and atmosphere (without it the FP rate is 34%).
