# TODO #5 — Multi-device sync is last-writer-wins and silently lossy under concurrency

*Full record extracted verbatim from TODO.md (Known issues, completed row) on 2026-09-01; the tracker row keeps the TLDR and the verdict. Table pipe escapes (`\|`) are unescaped here; `<br>` breaks became paragraphs.*

**Effort:** M · **Tier:** Fable

## Task

**Multi-device sync is last-writer-wins and silently lossy under concurrency** — the reconcile (`load()` in storage-adapter.js) adopts the server blob only when `serverTurn > localTurn`, with zero conflict detection. Two devices alternating politely is fine; play 3 turns offline on the phone then 1 turn online on desktop and whichever blob carries the higher turn number replaces the other WHOLESALE — the losing device's turns vanish with no warning, not even a toast. Cheap mitigation now: detect divergence (server turn ≠ local turn AND both changed since last sync — e.g. compare a per-device `lastSyncedTurn`) and at minimum warn + offer export before overwrite. Real fix is the server-authoritative model (Architecture decisions ▸ "State authority"), where this becomes a merge/lock question.
