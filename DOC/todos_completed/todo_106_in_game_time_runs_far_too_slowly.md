## Task

**In-game time runs far too slowly, and the clock silently loses most of what the narration describes.** TLDR: 216 turns of Day 1 play advanced the campaign clock 1043 minutes while the narration actually described ~2332 — the clock is running at under half the story's pace, and a day takes ~200 turns to pass. **Measured 2026-07-30** from the live save (`Ammut_t1265.tnd`, Rise of the Runelords) — full line-item evidence in [timeline_day1.html](timeline_day1.html) (216 rows, per-turn duration reconstructed from the narration under the GM's own reference scale, then compared against the real stored clock). ⚠ Day 1 of this campaign is **turns 1050–1265, not the campaign's first day** — the clock shipped at v1.389 (≈t917) and `migrateWorldState` starts an old save's clock at Day 0 on load ([state.js:254](state.js:254)), so the counter began mid-campaign; the t1049 `[REST:long]` rolled it to the 1440 dawn boundary that opens Day 1. **TWO INDEPENDENT CAUSES, both confirmed:** ① **the reference scale prices actions, not scenes** — [tag_table.js:68](tag_table.js:68) gives the GM "conversation 1-5 min", and conversation is **100 of 216 turns** (46% of play), so ~4 min/turn means a 16-hour waking day needs ~200 turns to fill. The scale times the words spoken; the walk over, the waiting, and the aftermath are free. ② **a turn with no `[TIME_ADVANCE:]` advances ZERO minutes, silently** — the handler opens `if(!ts.length)return;` with no warn and no floor, and `clockAdvance` has exactly one caller, so nothing backstops it. The visible symptom in the live save: the clock reads Day 1 17h23m (≈11pm) while `world.time` says "early morning" and the t1263 prose has "Sandpoint waking up around you both" — **the story slept and the clock did not**; the 5 turns classed as narrative time-skips alone carry 555 narrated minutes the clock had no way to receive. **② is the bigger and cheaper lever, and it is a no-silent-failures violation.** Candidate remedies (none ratified): a loud warn (and/or a minimum-1-minute floor) when a story turn advances nothing; a scene-level framing in the reference text ("charge the whole scene, including approach and aftermath"); rescaled category values; an explicit narrative time-skip tag for between-scene gaps. **⚠ Drift surface — the reference scale lives in the STATE TAGS doc block, byte-frozen by the suite (strip/doc hashes), and the tag vocabulary + prompt docs are named surface. Fable-tier, design review before code; changing the text is a deliberate hash re-baseline, not an edit.**
