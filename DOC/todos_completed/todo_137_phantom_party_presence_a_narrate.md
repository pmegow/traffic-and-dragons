## Task

**Phantom party presence — a narrated separation with no surviving `[PARTY_SPLIT:]` record makes every companion co-located.** Field case: Runelords t1467 ([Sol forensics](DOC/Research/OffTheRails_sol.html)). Daeris explicitly stayed at the Magnimar inn at t1443 and Morwen explicitly remained outside the Spire seam-door at t1457, yet both reached t1467 with no `charSheet.splitLoc`; Daeris then materialized inside at t1463 and durable memory now simultaneously says she stayed at the inn and fought in the Spire. The live separations were generated on v1.544/v1.546, before #135's v1.550 one-response grace, so a correct tag may have been written then immediately purged by #133b; raw tags are no longer retained, and GM non-emission produces the identical save, so the exact fork is honestly unproven. **Confirmed surviving class:** every audit (`partySplitMembers`, `buildSplitAudit`, SPLIT THREADS, #133c HP masking) starts from an existing negative override; if the record is absent, `partyMember` is treated as physical presence by `pcEffectiveLoc`, full PARTY MEMBER SHEETS call the companion “fighting alongside,” and `buildSceneManifest` unconditionally authorizes every party member as present. There is no write-side transition check and no way for #133/#135 to heal a missing record. **Remedy direction:** add a loud, neutral post-response compliance check for a narrated companion departure/stay-behind with no matching split mutation (engine detects, GM decides location; never auto-place from prose), retain enough tag/mutation provenance to adjudicate future field cases, and make party-sheet/scene-manifest presence derive from effective location instead of raw membership. Red-first field shapes: Daeris stays at inn then party leaves next response; Morwen seals a door from outside while the main party enters; explicit split survives and remote members are not presented as alongside; deliberately missing tag produces a visible correction obligation rather than phantom co-presence. ⚠ Drift surface (tag lifecycle, prompt presence, suggestion manifest, memory contamination).
