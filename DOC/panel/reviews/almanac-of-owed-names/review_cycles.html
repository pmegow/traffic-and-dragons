Outcome

Bellmere retains its grief, borrowed dead and permanent fog. The Compact now has a fixed chronology, specific permissions and disclosed costs; the mystery has stable evidence, and the climax offers distinct, consequential routes.

Published v1.0 Release Candidate as catalog revision 2; every field matches the reviewed source. The previous catalog entry was backed up. Existing campaigns keep their starting edition. Source commit is local; the shared-branch Git push awaits approval because unrelated location-display and test commits are ancestors.

Method

Three delegated passes apply five design lenses, four writing lenses and five composite player personas to the same snapshot at baseline and after each of three revision cycles. All fourteen perspectives reread each candidate. These are rubric applications, not participation by the named authors, interviews or human playtests. Source grades, evidence, mitigations and dissent remain visible below. No one-hour target was imposed on this campaign.

Changes and verification

  • One annual Setting occurs at the recorded third-night high tide. Act One's occupied chair is a separate household supper; postponing dinner cannot change expiry.
  • Entries, face licenses, threshold invitations and life/name pledges are distinct. Reading or mentioning a name does not bind anyone. Civic renewal costs one voluntarily pledged year of life; Perce's ten-year rejuvenations are separate.
  • The dead clerk's handwriting, Guest access, Corentin's omissions, Hesper's false entry and Perce's receipts have fixed causes. Independent records preserve essential information when witnesses refuse or disappear.
  • Wick is the original living child, with a specific mistaken claim and remedy. Borrowed bodies leave salt husks and do not resurrect buried humans; the Guest's ultimate origin and moral self-understanding remain uncertain.
  • Ordinary renewal can lawfully omit all dead names. Negotiation can further limit the living giver's licensed face. Severance ends protection at the next tide, whereas nonrenewal keeps the current term until its fixed expiry. Departure and prepared evacuation can work; the permanent fog survives.
