The Last Lantern-Seed

Three edit-and-review cycles · final v1.0 Release Candidate

Final blueprint Final snapshot Baseline Round 1 Round 2 Round 3

Outcome

The hopeful star-road journey retains its scope and wonder. The rite, seed stages, disguise and threatened waypoints now have fixed causes and usable alternatives. Three revision cycles resolved the editorial findings while retaining differing player preferences.

Prepared for publication after final checks; not yet published.

Method

Three delegated passes apply five design lenses, four writing lenses and five composite player personas to the same snapshot at baseline and after each of three revision cycles. All fourteen perspectives reread each candidate. These are rubric applications, not participation by the named authors, interviews or human playtests. Source grades, evidence, mitigations and dissent remain visible below. No one-hour target was imposed on this campaign.

Changes and verification

  • The seed is a dormant husk, then an awake closed bud, then a rooted open lantern. Light loss has visible causes, mitigation and honest loss outcomes.
  • The planting needs living light, the learned rite and any willing planter. The archive has readable instructions and observable access; Maren's copy plus trunk inscriptions offers an independent route.
  • Corwin is a living saboteur using a bounded glamour of Maren's dead partner Edris. His supposed bargain is his interpretation of silent visions. Trust, discovery and later opposition follow play. Pell has a concrete, limited observation.
  • Spire failure and the queen's vault crisis follow physical damage and neglected containment. Preparation, refusal, bypass and successful prevention matter.
  • Joined roots restore the network but need willing tenders at each waypoint. Severed roots concentrate a refuge maintainable by one community, losing the road. Both close the central breach; distant dark and unrepaired crossings have actual consequences.
  • Pre-1.0 draft promoted to v1.0 Release Candidate after the final review; metadata promotion changes no story content.

Four immutable snapshots each received all fourteen perspectives. Actual normalization and isolated import preserved 64 authored fields, five NPCs, six locations, five rules and nine arcs. Report links and desktop/mobile rendering were checked before shipment. The mandatory commit gate must pass. No narrated session or real-player test was run.
