The Iron Meridian

Three edit-and-review cycles · final v1.0 Release Candidate

Final blueprint Final snapshot Baseline Round 1 Round 2 Round 3

Outcome

The oppressive city, engineered indenture and leviathan crisis remain. Three revisions replace compulsory rescue, betrayal, confession and collapse with evidence, motivated bargains and consequential alternatives. Final review identified no further required editorial changes.

Published v1.0 Release Candidate as catalog revision 2; every field matches the reviewed source. The previous catalog entry was backed up. Existing campaigns keep their starting edition.

Method

Three delegated passes apply five design lenses, four writing lenses and five composite player personas to the same snapshot at baseline and after each of three revision cycles. All fourteen perspectives reread each candidate. These are rubric applications, not participation by the named authors, interviews or human playtests. Source grades, evidence, mitigations and dissent remain visible below. No one-hour target was imposed on this campaign.

Changes and verification

  • Optional Vessa bargain; independent purge survival, refusal and capture remain valid. Faction help follows disclosed terms and motives rather than inevitable betrayal.
  • Forged tags can work. Detection depends on observed mismatches and accessible records; creatures and ledger manipulation share those limits.
  • Engine trouble has a common coolant cause, repair and landing alternatives, and a charted dry refuge. Established skiff capacity, prior repairs and evacuation choices determine consequences.
  • Ferrin evidence can be obtained from independent sources. Death, refusal and missing clues permit unresolved culpability without blocking the engine decision. Clank and memory resonance have explicit bounds.
  • Creature prose shortened to the existing runtime cap. Pre-1.0 draft promoted to v1.0 Release Candidate; final promotion changes metadata only.

All 14 perspectives reviewed each of four immutable snapshots (baseline plus three revisions). Actual normalization and isolated import preserved every authored field, seven NPCs, five locations, six rules and ten arcs. Report checked at desktop and 320/390px widths. The mandatory commit gate passed all standalone suites and 3,106 engine assertions. No model narration or real player test was performed. A first test edit used Redd instead of Redd Salter; the full name was corrected before the passing run. Publication was held for an unrelated in-flight memory proof failure until its workstream repaired it; no unrelated changes were included.
