The Riverlight Masquerade

Player panel · five composite personas

Other panel report Readiness audit and test plan Reviewed blueprint

Recommendation

Strong fit for players who enjoy an evidence-led mystery; short-session accessibility still needs observation. The clearest wording improvement is Act Two's “Two corroborating routes”: say corroborating evidence and name the existing alternatives, so it cannot suggest completing two whole branches.

Offer one brief, optional player-authored closing action before asking whether to stop. Test re-entry after interruptions, what each discovered document proves, actual document custody and honest closure of skipped leads.

Keep the dissent: Bert's accumulation preference and Nan's cozy preference are imperfect genre fits. They do not justify adding loot systems or removing the murder. Ines's appetite for depth can coexist with Rosa's quick exit.

Scope and method

Three delegated review passes applied nine documented design/writing lenses and five composite player personas to the same v1.0 Release Candidate. These are rubric-based assessments, not participation by the named authors, interviews, votes or human playtests. This report contains 5 perspectives. Findings distinguish editorial opportunities, taste disagreements and unverified runtime risks. Existing mitigations remain visible.

The stored rubrics supply source grades: ● primary, ◐ secondary, ○ inferred. These grades describe provenance, not certainty that a proposed change will improve play. Inferred preferences are not used as verdicts. Player evidence from other formats does not establish text-game behavior.

Panel synthesis

Player personas

Agreement

  • The current no-Mira route is credible on paper: coat plus authenticated commission; losing Mira must not force a chase.
  • Fixed truth, actual custody and honest partial endings are strengths across applicable lenses.
  • One hour remains a test target. Observe real newcomer comprehension and separately test interrupted play and declined leads.

Disagreement

  • Rosa/Nan prioritize re-entry and completed short chunks; Ines welcomes optional depth. Preserve both.
  • Bert's accumulation and Nan's cozy preferences are partial or poor genre fits, not reasons to add an economy or remove the stakes.
  • Gerry's preferred epilogue authorship should be brief and optional for players who want to stop.

Recommended next steps

  • Clarify Act Two's two corroborating routes as corroborating evidence with explicit alternatives.
  • Invite an optional one-sentence player epilogue contribution.
  • Verify no-Mira settlement, evidence custody after handover, interrupted recall of names/evidence/next action, fictional deadlines across pauses, and honest unresolved murder. Add no UI or mechanics solely from this paper review.

Recommended order: clarify the scheme and evidence wording; consider the optional closing contribution; then run the blind newcomer test and directed route checks. Small characterization additions are optional. Keep task 446 open until actual play evidence is recorded.
