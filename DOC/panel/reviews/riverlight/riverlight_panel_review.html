The Riverlight Masquerade

Design and writing panel · nine lenses

Other panel report Readiness audit and test plan Reviewed blueprint

Recommendation

Keep the compact political mystery. Clarify one causal link before adding more material. The strongest content concern is Devrin's expectation: excluding Petrell supposedly makes his barges indispensable, but the alliance needs all five holds' assent. Specify what exclusion means and why he expects the remaining arrangement to benefit him. His plan may fail; the issue is an under-explained expectation, not an impossible plot.

Optional craft improvements fit in existing beats: one ordinary detail about Orris, one visible freight-cost consequence, and one self-serving belief for Devrin. No extra locations or required scenes are recommended.

Preserve the fixed truth, early settlement, evidence route without Mira and honest partial endings. Eight arc records remain a model-compliance check, not proof of eight compulsory scenes. Approximately one hour is a test target, not an observed or advertised duration.

Scope and method

Three delegated review passes applied nine documented design/writing lenses and five composite player personas to the same v1.0 Release Candidate. These are rubric-based assessments, not participation by the named authors, interviews, votes or human playtests. This report contains 9 perspectives. Findings distinguish editorial opportunities, taste disagreements and unverified runtime risks. Existing mitigations remain visible.

The stored rubrics supply source grades: ● primary, ◐ secondary, ○ inferred. These grades describe provenance, not certainty that a proposed change will improve play. Inferred preferences are not used as verdicts. Player evidence from other formats does not establish text-game behavior.
